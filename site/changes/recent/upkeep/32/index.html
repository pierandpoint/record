    Record pages written for the 6 first Mission Bay records (Block 12 West, the Public Safety Building, HomeRise/Block 9, Block 9A, Mission Bay School, Chase Center).     14 OpenStreetMap building footprints added for UCSF's Mission Bay campus: the hospital complex housing Benioff Children's, Bakar Cancer and Betty Irene Moore Women's Hospital; 6 research and academic buildings (Genentech Hall, Rock Hall, Byers Hall, Sandler Neurosciences Center, Smith Cardiovascular Research Building, Helen Diller Family Cancer Research Building); Mission Hall; the William J. One gap between Genentech Hall and Byers Hall is explained by DataSF curb data.

    OpenStreetMap building footprints; DataSF Parcels - Active and Retired (https://data.sf.gov/d/acdm-wktn); DataSF Curbs and Islands (https://data.sfgov.org/dataset/Curbs-and-Islands/emxt-b6yg)
