  • Recorded Sep 22, 2026CoverageAll sites

    Blocks 24A/B and 24C corrected to Genentech Hall and Byers Hall. Both OCII's Dec 2019 Land Use Map and UCSF's June 2025 CAC presentation (map updated December 2024) show 24A/B and 24C in the blue UCSF row directly south of Koret Quad (22)/Campus Way, exactly where Genentech Hall and Byers Hall sit.   • Recorded Sep 22, 2026CoverageAll sites

    490 Illinois St (Valley Center for Vision) and 499 Illinois St (Alexandria Center for Science and Technology) merged into one record under OCII block X4. Both share the same DataSF Building Footprints identifier (mblr SF3940003), one Assessor block-lot pairing, and commercial listings describe them together as a connected 490-499 Illinois campus, so tracking them separately double-counted one real estate holding. They are combined into one map shape (the two building outlines stay distinct, ~12m apart, not touching). Also confirmed at the time: OCII's Dec 2019 Land Use Map and UCSF's own numbering agree that blocks 33/34 (the unnamed buildings between 3rd St and Illinois St, corrected 2026-09-20) are a separate location from 490/499 Illinois St (X4, east of Illinois St), so no renumbering was needed, resolving the apparent 33/34 conflict with UCSF's June 2025 CAC presentation. A September 2026 accuracy check noted that this conclusion about blocks 33/34 was reversed the same day (see the correction that splits block 34 and identifies block 33): UCSF's own map labels block 33 'Valley Center for Vision', the same name as the 490 Illinois building. The 'one Assessor block-lot pairing' via mblr SF3940003 was not re-derived; the 499 Illinois record cites Assessor parcel 3940004.

    DataSF Building Footprints (https://data.sfgov.org/Geographic-Locations-and-Boundaries/Building-Footprints/ynuv-fyni); OCII, Mission Bay Land Use Map, 2019-12 (https://sfocii.org/sites/default/files/inline-files/Att%201%20Mission%20Bay%20Land%20Use%20Map.pdf); UCSF Mission Bay Development Overview, Mission Bay CAC presentation, Jun 12, 2025 (https://sfocii.org/sites/default/files/2025-06/Item%202%20-%20UCSF%20Mission%20Bay%20Development%20Overview.pdf)

  • Recorded Sep 22, 2026CoverageAll sites

    An assumption made earlier on 2026-09-22, that blocks 33/34 were unrelated to 490/499 Illinois St, was corrected: UCSF built three structures here where the OCII plan showed two. OCII plan block 33 is UCSF's Valley Center for Vision, a second, physically distinct building from the already-tracked 490 Illinois St tower of the same name, about 60-100m east across Illinois St; how the two relate is left as an open question, not asserted as either the same facility or unrelated. OCII plan block 34 was itself two buildings, not one: it is split along a visible roofline seam in satellite imagery into the UCSF Bayfront Medical Building (north, 520 Illinois St) and the UCSF Illinois Street Garage (south, 590 Illinois St, 9 stories/500 spaces). Both were already documented in earlier research, which had guessed the split the other way round (garage as 33, clinic as 34) before an on-the-ground correction. The 33/34 boundary and the new Bayfront/Garage boundary were both redrawn from block 34's own straight east and west edges (the Illinois St and 3rd St sides, bearing about -85.6 degrees) by line intersection, rather than the original flat-latitude cut. The Mariposa St edge was left as originally traced (already within about 1 degree of the same angle).

    UCSF Mission Bay Development Overview, Mission Bay CAC presentation, Jun 12, 2025 (https://sfocii.org/sites/default/files/2025-06/Item%202%20-%20UCSF%20Mission%20Bay%20Development%20Overview.pdf); UCSF Real Estate, Bayfront Medical Building and Illinois Street Garage (https://realestate.ucsf.edu/projects/bayfront-medical-building-and-illinois-street-garage); Satellite imagery, Pier & Point's own reading (imagery provider not recorded)
