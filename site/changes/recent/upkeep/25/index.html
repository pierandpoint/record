  • Recorded Sep 24, 2026CorrectionMission Bay

    Two tenant entries were dropped as duplicates, not errors. Permits 201911016171 (two illuminated wall signs) and 201911016173 (one illuminated wall sign) were the same signage buildout for the same Bank of America branch at 588 Mission Bay Blvd North, filed minutes apart on 2019-11-01 and both issued 2019-11-12; the more specific entry was kept. Likewise permits 202205194649 (sign A) and 202205194650 (sign B) were the same Proper Food(s) signage buildout at the same address, filed 12 minutes apart on 2022-09-01; the 'Proper Foods' entry (open, dated) was kept and the 'Proper Food' entry (announced, undated) dropped, rather than show the same tenant twice under two spellings and two statuses. Permits are read one at a time, with no check for duplicates across permits, so this is a known gap rather than a one-off mistake. View record →
