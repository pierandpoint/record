    A question about why the map showed plan block 7E but not 7W led to a re-check of how accurately the plan drawing lines up with the real map this far out. The alignment, fit from 6 control points clustered in the South campus core, drifts enough here that the 7W label lands inside a different, adjacent block's building: 1300 Fourth Street (Mission Bay South Block 6 East), a 142-unit TNDC affordable housing development (plus a manager's unit and a mid-block mews of 12 townhomes/10 flats; SocketSite's Aug 2016 'about to break ground' item anticipated groundbreaking in 2016) that was not previously tracked. It was confirmed via its own Assessor parcel (parcel 8711505) and DataSF's Historical Secured Property Tax Roll. DataSF's Building Footprints layer captures only small roof-edge fragments of this building (confirmed against satellite imagery), so the record uses the Assessor parcel boundary instead, as a parcel-backed record rather than an approximate trace. Plan-label positions this far from the control points cannot be trusted to identify which building a label refers to without an independent check (here, the DataSF Assessor tax roll), which is the caveat under which the alignment was adopted.
