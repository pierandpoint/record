  • Recorded Sep 23, 2026CoverageAll sites

    DataSF's Assessor Parcel dataset gives Chase Center (parcel 8722025) and the neighboring Uber Mission Bay 3 & 4 office towers (parcel 8722063) identical geometry: one shared master-condominium-association land parcel covering the whole Mission Bay South Blocks 29-32 superblock (Chase Center, both Uber towers, a parking structure and a small broadcast building are all separate condo units on one shared plat). That made the two records' shapes overlap completely on the map, so a click anywhere in the shared area could open either record, and each record's own location map showed the wrong footprint. Both now use non-overlapping shapes built from real data: Chase Center is the full Assessor parcel with both Uber towers' OpenStreetMap building outlines subtracted, and Uber Mission Bay 3 & 4 is the union of those same two outlines. Harmonic Brewing's standalone taproom building, at the northeast edge of the site (7 Warriors Way Ste 206 per its DataSF business-registration address; the registry's geocode puts Harmonic Brewing, Miller & Lux and Cupcakin' at the same point, so it does not place any of them in a particular building), and Miller & Lux (7 Warriors Way Ste 208 per its registration address; its position inside the arena footprint is not established by the registry) are both Chase Center/Thrive City tenants, not separate structures, so Harmonic Brewing's building is included in Chase Center's new shape rather than tracked separately. Re-matching business registrations against the corrected shape turned up three more Thrive City registrations: Cupcakin' Bake Shop (Ste 200), 'Siren SF' (1 Warriors Way Unit 24, shown as Siren Cafe & Bar) and The Sarap Shop (1 Warriors Way). These are registrations at Chase Center addresses, not proof of open storefronts, and each has its own entry.

    DataSF Parcels (https://data.sf.gov/d/acdm-wktn); OpenStreetMap way 1410182008 (https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1410182008); OpenStreetMap way 1410183655 (https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1410183655); DataSF Registered Business Locations (https://data.sfgov.org/Economy-and-Community/Registered-Business-Locations-San-Francisco/g8m3-pdis); Chase Center, Thrive City (https://www.chasecenter.com/thrive-city/)
