  • Recorded Oct 10, 2026CorrectionMission Bay

    Block 18C is now named 'Mission Bay South Block 18C (UCSF Child Development Center)'. Its identification as the UCSF at Mission Bay Child Development Center, run by Bright Horizons at 727 Nelson Rising Lane, had rested on an on-the-ground observation; it is now sourced. Bright Horizons' own page and the City's business registrations place the centre at that address, the City's address point for 727 Nelson Rising Lane falls inside this shape (about 2.7 m from its edge; the nearest other mapped block is about 20 m away), and UCSF's June 2025 presentation gives the block's near-term use as a childcare center. The address point marks the street frontage, not the building, and the address also carries another business registration, so it is not exclusive to this building. No source gives an opening date. Shape, status and use are unchanged; the old page address now redirects to the new one. View record →

    Bright Horizons, UCSF at Mission Bay Child Development Center (https://www.brighthorizons.com/daycare-centers/ca/san-francisco/727-nelson-rising-lane-94143); DataSF Registered Business Locations, ttxid 1367181-07-241 (https://data.sfgov.org/resource/g8m3-pdis.json?ttxid=1367181-07-241); DataSF Addresses, address ID 743045 (https://data.sfgov.org/resource/3mea-di5p.json?eas_address_id=743045); UCSF Mission Bay Development Overview, Mission Bay CAC presentation, Jun 12, 2025 (https://sfocii.org/sites/default/files/2025-06/Item%202%20-%20UCSF%20Mission%20Bay%20Development%20Overview.pdf)
