  • Jun 9, 2026MilestoneMission Rock

    Mission Rock Block D1: Port Commission approves Resolution 26-34, authorizing up to $10 million in Port Capital as pre-development funding for Phase 2 (for eligible Phase 2 pre-development costs, including 'design and engineering for Phase 2 infrastructure; Developer, Port, and City staff costs; consultant expenses'; the memo separately says the Developer will advance pre-development and design work for the vertical buildings in parallel), intended to avoid a 12-18 month delay to construction readiness. Staff's schedule lists a 'Potential Construction Start' of Summer/Fall 2027 for horizontal infrastructure and vertical building construction, and the memo says Phase 2 could begin construction 'as early as 2027' once anchor tenants are secured; its Table 1 gives illustrative repayment scenarios for a 2027, 2028 or 2029 vertical start – not a committed date for this or any other Phase 2 block.

    Port of San Francisco, Jun 5, 2026; Port of San Francisco, Jun 9, 2026

  • Apr 14, 2026MilestoneMission Bay

    Mission Bay South Block 4 East: A CEQA Notice of Exemption is filed for the Block 4-East project (statutory exemption under AB 1449 / Public Resources Code section 21080.40, the 100%-affordable-housing exemption). The notice states that all residential units "will be dedicated to lower-income households (earning no more than 70% of Area Median Income" on a HUD and TCAC basis, excluding unrestricted manager units, and that the project "is subject to recorded TCAC Regulatory Agreements prior to the issuance of building permits" (future tense: the agreements are to be recorded). Resolution No. 02-2026 gives two manager's units in each phase.

    Office of Community Investment and Infrastructure (OCII), Apr 14, 2026
