    The Regents of the University of California, 2000-05
  • May 2000StatusMission Bay

    William J. Rutter Center: UC Regents item for the Committee on Grounds and Buildings (May 2000) recommends certifying an EIR addendum and approving the design of the Mission Bay Campus Community Center Building (21B) – this building, confirmed by matching architects (MBT Architecture as executive architect with Legorreta Arquitectos as design subconsultant) and program (fitness/recreation center, conference center, food service, retail); the item recounts that the Regents approved a $58,000,000 total project cost in March 2000 and states construction 'will begin in 2001 and be completed in 2002'. The item is the recommendation; the committee's action itself is not shown
