    Pier 70 Building 102 (former Power House): Built as the Union Iron Works powerhouse to Charles Peter Weeks's design; equipment dating from 1913-1914 remained in 2013

  • ~1901MilestonePotrero Station

    Potrero Power Station Block 15 / Station A: Station A construction begins (completed 1902, with a 1930-31 addition); individually eligible for the California Register under Criterion 1, and a contributor to the Third Street Industrial District

    SF Planning Department, 2018-10
  • ~1901StatusPotrero Station

    Potrero Power Station Block 15 / Station A: Station A's original construction begins (completed 1902, with a 1930-31 addition), predating the Potrero Power Station redevelopment plan by more than a century; the building is retained today (rather than demolished) for adaptive reuse as an office building

    SF Planning Department, 2018-10; SF Planning Department, 2022-03-02
  • ~1900StatusPier 70

    Pier 70 Building 21 (Substation No. 5, relocated): Built circa 1900 as part of the Risdon Iron and Locomotive Works (shown as a machine shop and transformer house on the 1914 and 1936 Sanborn maps)

    National Park Service / California Office of Historic Preservation (nomination prepared for the Port of San Francisco by Carey & Co.), 2014-04-17
  • 1896StatusPier 70

    Pier 70 Building 104 (UIW Headquarters): Built as the Union Iron Works office building, designed by George Percy and Frederick Hamilton

    National Park Service / California Office of Historic Preservation (nomination prepared for the Port of San Francisco by Carey & Co.), 2014-04-17
  • 1885StatusPier 70

    Pier 70 Building 113/114/115/116 (Union Iron Works Machine Shop): Eastern portion of Building 113/114 (blacksmith and boiler shop) completed to civil engineer D. E. Melliss's design; western portion (machine shop) completed 1886

    National Park Service / California Office of Historic Preservation (nomination prepared for the Port of San Francisco by Carey & Co.), 2014-04-17
