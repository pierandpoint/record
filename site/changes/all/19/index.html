  • ~Aug 2018MilestoneMission Bay

    Parklab Gardens & Mission Bay Commons (interim, west): Parklab Gardens (one interim installation inside the mapped Parklab park strip) is reported as "opening Friday" in an SF Chronicle article of Aug 17, 2018 that pairs it with Stagecoach Greens mini-golf; the article is a pre-opening report, so the month is approximate and no later source gives the opening day. OCII's Nov 2024 update labels P15 "Parklab Gardens & Stagecoach Greens" on its people-counts map (PDF pp.17-18) but groups "P13 & P15: Food Truck Park with Outdoor Dining (SPARK Social SF & Parklab Gardens)" on a survey slide (p.31), so which parcel it sits on is not settled. Interim-use pilots on the Commons parcels began on P13 in 2016, P15 in 2017 and P12 in 2018 (SF Rec & Park Workshop 3 deck, p.15).

    SF Chronicle, "Outdoor mini golf right here in SF? Yes, and with food trucks and a beer garden" (Amy Graff, Aug 17, 2018; read as republished at https://stagecoachgreens.com/cms/outdoor-mini-golf-right-here-in-sf-yes-and-with-food-trucks-and-a-beer-garden/); OCII Mission Bay Future Parks CAC Update, Nov 14, 2024 (https://sfocii.org/sites/default/files/2024-11/Item%205%20-%20Remaining%20Mission%20Bay%20Open%20Space%20Parcels%20Update.pdf); https://sfrecpark.org/DocumentCenter/View/25986/20250429-PRESENTATION-Workshop-3
