    DataSF's Assessor Parcel dataset gives Chase Center (parcel 8722025) and the neighboring Uber Mission Bay 3 & 4 office towers (parcel 8722063) identical geometry: one shared master-condominium-association land parcel covering the whole Mission Bay South Blocks 29-32 superblock (Chase Center, both Uber towers, a parking structure and a small broadcast building are all separate condo units on one shared plat). Re-matching business registrations against the corrected shape turned up three more Thrive City registrations: Cupcakin' Bake Shop (Ste 200), 'Siren SF' (1 Warriors Way Unit 24, shown as Siren Cafe & Bar) and The Sarap Shop (1 Warriors Way). These are registrations at Chase Center addresses, not proof of open storefronts, and each has its own entry.
