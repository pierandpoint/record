  • Oct 13, 2026CorrectionMission Rock

    The Design Controls permit only structured parking on Block D2 but require the structure to provide at least 10,000 square feet of active uses and/or transit-related services at the ground floor. The 97,498 sq ft area is the March 2012 proposal's figure for Block D as a whole, before the 2018 Design Controls divided it into D1 and D2. The Port's June 2026 report schedules the Developer's Phase 2 Submittal for Winter/Spring 2027 and a possible construction start for Summer/Fall 2027 (expected dates); no source read reports construction or a vertical design submittal for this block or states its current use. The Port's October 2023 memo says Phase 2 will include the parking garage but does not name Parcel D2. The Board of Supervisors' own records date the Mission Rock DDA approval (Resolution 42-18) to February 13, 2018 and the final passage of the Development Agreement ordinance (No. 33-18) to February 27, 2018; the Notice of Determination gives March 6, 2018 as the City's approval date and lists the Board's EIR action (File 171286) among its final actions. The Port's fact sheet lists August 15, 2018 as the Development Agreement's effective date, and the Planning page dates the DDA 8/15/2018. The Port's January 2018 staff report describes the DDA's outside dates for starting later phases (a legal deadline, not a forecast); the DDA text itself has not been read, so no calendar date is given. DDA-specific section numbers and target dates previously listed here are removed because the DDA text has not been read. View record →
