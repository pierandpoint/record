  • Sep 22, 2026MilestoneAll sites

    A second set of research claims about the 16 records that had deliberately been left undated was verified. Confirmed and added: Mission Bay Block 34 gained a May 2020 UC Regents preliminary-plans item and its August 2024 completion date firmed to the exact day (Aug 27), while a claimed April 2021 garage-construction-start date was checked and rejected, because a Commercial Property Executive article directly contradicts it, showing the garage broke ground after, not before, the clinical building. Park P10 gained two real earlier-stage milestones from a directly read EPA/DTSC brownfields fact sheet that names "Mission Bay Park P10" specifically, and a 2011 press-tour article; a companion Board of Supervisors citation given for a different record (P11/P11A) turned out, on direct Legistar lookup, to refer to an unrelated Graffiti Advisory Board appointment and was rejected outright. Avalon at Mission Bay II gained a real Q1 2005 construction-start date from the developer's own SEC-filed earnings release. 255 Berry's existing 2004 completion milestone had its source upgraded from a generic listing search to the developer's own project page. Mission Creek Park (the P3 extension) gained an October 2022 completion date from a Landscape Performance Series case study, answering an open question already noted on the record (whether a fall-2021 target opening actually happened; it slipped a year further). For two records (the P26 interchange wedge and Block 22) the claimed dates were confirmed as flat misattributions: the cited Port Commission/Lotus Water material is entirely about the already-tracked Bayfront Park (P22), not either record, so nothing was added to either. Five more records (The Beacon, Mission Bay Commons, the Creek Park's own P1-P3 segment as distinct from P3, and the three UCSF garage/utility-plant records) returned no independently confirmable date and were left as they were, several because realestate.ucsf.edu persistently blocks automated access. Superseded the same day: Clark Construction's own press release states the garage portion started in April 2021, so the April 2021 date rejected above was restored by the next set of research checks. A September 2026 accuracy check noted that the outcomes listed here for P11/P11A, Avalon II, 255 Berry, Mission Creek Park P3 and P26/Block 22 come from Legistar, SEC and Landscape Performance Series pages that were not re-read in that check, and that the May 2020 Regents item and Aug 27, 2024 completion for Block 34 could not be read (realestate.ucsf.edu, stantec.com and commercialsearch.com return 403).

    A third set of research claims was verified, under ground rules set after the two earlier sets. Citations were real and quotable far more often this time, but two of the 13 claims were still rejected before verification began, because both cited real documents describing a different physical parcel from the one tracked here (Block 22 vs. Bayfront Park/P22, confirmed about 500m apart and matching UCSF's own "Koret Quad" label; the P26 interchange wedge vs. the real, separately named Mariposa Park, confirmed through San Francisco's Legistar API, where the cited ordinance's own title places it on a different street). Of the remaining 11, confirmed and added: Uber Mission Bay 3 & 4 gained two 2018 structural-steel milestones (Mortenson's own steel-fabrication page, distinct from the arena's steel package) and a joint-venture formation note (Alexandria Real Estate's own SEC filing); Mission Bay Commons gained a narrower, accurately worded Nov 2011 completion milestone for Park P16 specifically (the source names only P16, not P17, and says "recently completed," not "dedicated," as the fuller claim implied); Mission Bay Block 34 gained a real April 2021 garage-construction-start date from Clark Construction's own press release, reversing the earlier rejection of the same date (which had relied on a vaguer secondary paraphrase). Caught and rejected: P11/P11A's claimed Board of Supervisors File #190755 was re-checked against Legistar's API and confirmed, again, not to exist; UCSF's 1670 Owens Garage citation turned out to be about a different, separately addressed UCSF garage (1625 Owens St, serving the Rutter Center) three streets away. Flagged, not resolved: whether the Mission Creek Park P1-P3 record actually belongs to Mission Bay North's own NP1/NP2 open-space system rather than the Mission Bay South labels it currently carries. Left as they were: the UCSF Preliminary Utilities Plant and part of the Third Street Garage's completion date, both blocked because realestate.ucsf.edu refused every access attempt, including through the Wayback Machine, which was itself unreachable. The Third Street Garage did gain a general-contractor fact (Overaa Construction) confirming its identity and giving 882 parking stalls (the contractor's own project page); an 822-space figure from a 2003 pre-construction budget item was cited but could not be found in the September 2026 accuracy check, so the discrepancy is unconfirmed.
