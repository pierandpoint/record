Research notes

Shared Public Way paseo (Dr Maya Angelou Lane, north segment) -- the pedestrian paseo between Block A/The Canyon and Block G/Visa, from Exposition Street (Toni Stone Crossing) to China Basin Park; Design Controls Figure 3.1.1 marks it as Paseo 4.2. Built with Phase 1 ('a pedestrian paseo in between Parcels A and G', 2019 Phase 1 submittal; the submittal says Phase 1 will complete the Shared Public Way and Bridgeview Street paseos north of Exposition Street). Its own real Assessor lot, 8719B001 (1,145 m2, recorded 2020-06-12); DataSF's street centerline names it Dr Maya Angelou Ln (CNN 15286000, layer PRIVATE). Added 2026-09-17 (Mission Rock geometry brief).
