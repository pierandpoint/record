Sites/Mission Rock/Building B

Building B

Commercial · Mission Rock

Also known as Mission Rock Parcel B, 1090 Dr. Maya Angelou Lane

WORKac's 8-story commercial building on Parcel B, built with Tishman Speyer's life-science arm Breakthrough Properties as lab-ready space and marketed as Class A office/life science after its June 2023 TCO. It houses the district's blackwater recycling plant and has since leased to the Golden State Warriors (71,000 sq ft, October 2024), Coinbase (150,000 sq ft, 2025) and Nvidia (45,000 sq ft, November 2025).10, 11, 19, 4, 17, 3

Research notes

Building B -- ~300,000 sf Class A office/life-science (258,994 sf office + 13,773 sf retail); TCO June 2023; tenants incl. Coinbase, Nvidia (45,000 sf, lease starting ~Mar 2026); also houses the district's blackwater/non-potable water treatment plant (Port memo, Oct 2023; The Real Deal, Nov 2025; nibbi.com/projects/mission-rock-building-b). CORRECTED 2026-09-13: an earlier version of this row joined blklot 8719001 (the Wharfside/Berry Building at 185 Berry St, unrelated to Mission Rock -- see Building G's row above for the same error). Re-identified using the Mission Rock Design Controls' own Figure 1.1 (Land Use Plan) and Figure 6.2 (Height Plan): the official masterplan places Block B directly south of Block A along 3rd St, as a Commercial Mixed Use block -- matching blklot 8719C003, addressed 1089 3rd St (a higher address number than Canyon/Block A's 1011-1023, consistent with being one block further south), with a ~64,600 sqft multi-wing footprint plausible for this building's size, positioned in a 2x2 cluster with Canyon (8719B002, NW), Visa/Building G (8719A008, NE), and Verde (8719C001, SE) that matches the diagram's A/G (north row) and B/F (south row) layout exactly.
