Open questions

Looked for, not found

  • Whether Block J will be built residential or commercial has not been decided in any document read.
  • No construction start, Phase 2 budget approval or vertical design submittal for this block was found in Port Commission agendas through February 2026; the block remains part of the Seawall Lot 337 surface parking lot (Lot A).
  • Which blocks the December 2022 Phase 2 Submittal covers is not stated in the Port memos read; only the Phase 2 parking garage is named.
  • The DDA 'Outside Dates' for commencing later phases are in the DDA itself, which was not read.
  • No cost, developer capital or land-value figure exists for this block; the Port says Phase 2 appraisals are deferred until the market supports the minimum land values.
  • Images: no rendering specific to this block exists in the public-record documents read (the Design Controls give only diagrammatic envelopes), and the 2026 aerial serves as the 'before'/'now' view of the parking lot it sits in.
  • No expected-completion date could be sourced for Block J. The Mission Rock Design Controls (208 pages, checked in full) contain no phasing schedule at all. The DDA itself (lodged with the Board of Supervisors 2/1/2018, Board File 180092, adopted as Resolution 42-18, effective 8/15/2018) places Parcel J in Phase 4 in its Land Use Table (DDA Section 1.5) and its Exhibit B2 Schedule of Performance gives a 2018 'Target Date' of February 2025 for completing Phase 4's horizontal Phase Improvements -- already elapsed with the earlier Phase 2 still not begun as of the October 2023 'not currently financially feasible' finding in this record's own milestones. No later Port memo, Design Controls revision or news source gives a revised target year for Phase 4 or for the Project's full buildout, so no basis (record, phase or project) supports a non-elapsed date for this block.

About this shape

Accuracy

APPROXIMATE (planned, unbuilt block): no Assessor lot of its own yet. It sits on lot 8719A006, the ~37,400 m2 undivided remainder of Seawall Lot 337 that also holds Blocks C, D, E, H, I, J, Mission Rock Square, Channel Lane and the unbuilt internal streets. Traced 2026-09-17 from the adopted Design Controls' Figure 6.2 through a transform with 0.7 m RMS residual on 16 real lot corners, with vertices snapped to recorded lot lines where the figure draws the block against one (see digitization_method): treat street-facing and lot-line edges as accurate to ~1 m and interior edges (against unbuilt streets and open spaces) to ~1-3 m. The block's shape is the Design Controls' block, not a building footprint; a building may sit back from these lines.

Traced from

Mission Rock Design Controls (Port of San Francisco / Mission Rock Partners, adopted April 24, 2018), Figure 6.2 - Maximum Height Plan (p.151); land use from Figure 1.1 - Land Use Plan (p.10); block corners snapped to the recorded DataSF Assessor lot lines of the 2020 Seawall Lot 337 subdivision where they fall within 3 m.

Why there’s no DataSF parcel

A real Assessor lot exists under this block -- 8719A006, the undivided remainder of Seawall Lot 337 recorded 2020-06-12 -- but it has not been subdivided per block, so joining status.csv to it would paint the same ~9-acre parking-lot shape for every unbuilt block. Hand-derived geometry is used instead, with the lot's own lines wherever this block touches them.

Planning context

Flex block (Residential MU or Commercial MU), max height 90 ft commercial / 120 ft residential. Sits one row north of Block I, adjacent to Block F -- it does NOT front Mission Rock Square directly, despite some secondary summaries grouping it with the square's surrounding blocks.

Research notes

Approximate polygon for a real, D4D-adopted future block -- not yet built, no distinct real parcel yet. See accuracy_caveat.
