Commercial · Potrero Power Station

Also known as Block 11

Planned office/laboratory building on the Potrero Power Station masterplan, paired with Block 12 as a combined ~430,000 sq ft life-science campus; design under review as of the March 2025 annual compliance report, with no construction start found.3, 5

Open questions

Looked for, not found

  • No design review approval or construction-start date found for Block 11; the most recent primary source found (the 2024 annual compliance report) describes design as 'under review', not approved.
  • The combined 430,000 sq ft figure for Blocks 11+12 comes from the project's MEP engineer, not a Planning Department document; no Planning Commission or Planning Director approval memo for Block 11 was found to confirm or break out this figure.
  • The Chronicle's July 2026 report of a proposed +15 ft height increase is tied to a pending Development Agreement/Planning Code amendment (BOS Files 260724 and 260770) that had not been adopted as of this dossier's last-verified date.

About this shape

Accuracy

APPROXIMATE -- this block is not yet built and does not correspond to any subdivided Assessor parcel: it currently sits on vacant/graded land within the former power station site (unaddressed in DataSF's Assessor roll). Traced from an adopted regulatory diagram via multi-point real-street georeferencing (see digitization_method), but this diagram is dated 2020 and may not reflect later design changes. Treat vertex positions as accurate to roughly 10-25m, not survey-grade. South edge corrected 2026-09-16 (second pass) using the source diagram's own measured depth rather than a satellite curb search (see digitization_method) -- the diagram-derived depth should be reliable to within a few meters, but this edge has NOT been independently confirmed against a visible real-world curb. This block's WEST-EAST WIDTH is also known to disagree with the diagram (~55m actual vs ~92m implied) and has NOT been corrected -- flagged as an open follow-up, not resolved. Rebuilt 2026-09-16 via GDAL GCP/TPS georeferencing -- see digitization_method. Re-measured 2026-09-16 (third pass) in a single consistent pixel-detection pass across the whole block layout -- see digitization_method. Re-corrected 2026-09-16 (fourth pass) after fixing a mis-measured GCP -- see digitization_method.

Traced from

Potrero Power Station Design for Development, Figure 6.2.3 - Building Height Plan (p.245).

Why there’s no DataSF parcel

A real Assessor parcel may exist under this block (part of the site's larger, largely unaddressed footprint), but it has not been subdivided per the masterplan's individual named blocks -- this uses hand-traced approximate geometry instead.

Planning context

Flat 130 ft max height per Figure 6.2.3, within an 85-145 ft range sometimes cited secondhand. The D4D's Permitted Uses table confirms Lab/Life Science uses are permitted here (paired with Block 12 as a life-science campus in some secondary sources, combined at roughly 430,000 sq ft per a Keyser Marston Associates estimate -- notably less than a sometimes-cited '~500,000 GSF' figure). No source found specifically tying Herzog & de Meuron to this block (their confirmed Potrero work is Blocks 2 and 15/Station A). No construction-start source found.
