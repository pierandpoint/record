What’s next

~Dec 2026

Expected, not confirmed

Port Commission: Port staff and the developer are drafting amendments to the DDA and related transaction documents "for consideration by the Port Commission and Board of Supervisors by the end of this year or early next year" (Item 11A, memo of Aug 7, 2026, p.2 and p.6). The source gives no hearing date. The date shown is the early end of that window (December 2026); "early next year" falls in 2027. The same report proposes extending the term of the DDA, Development Agreement and Master Lease by 5 years.4

The waterfront arts-building parcel east of Building 21: excluded from the developer's purchase option, capped at 50 ft and five stories, and reserved under the 2017 DDA for an arts facility of up to 90,000 sq ft (with replacement studios for the Noonan Building artists) on a 50-year, $1-a-year lease once $17.5 million in private contributions is raised. The Port's proposed 2026 amendments would rezone it to allow housing if the arts facility proves infeasible.3, 4, 6, 7

About this shape

Accuracy

The outline is Assessor lot 4052011, so its edges are recorded lot lines, not a trace. The lot is Pier & Point's reading of the plan diagram and the satellite imagery (the lot lies just east of Building 21, where the D4D draws E4); no document read names it. A building may sit back from the lot lines. No vertical construction is reported on this block (Port Item 11A, Aug 7, 2026: construction of Phase 1 vertical projects and parks has not moved forward, and the developer has not exercised an option on any Phase 1 option parcel). Port Items 9B (Sep 22, 2017) and 12B (Feb 7, 2020) list Parcel E4 (the arts building parcel) among the parcels excluded from the developer's option under the Disposition and Development Agreement (DDA); Port Item 11A describes the option more generally, as covering 'all development parcels within the 28-Acre Site'.

Traced from

DataSF Assessor parcels (acdm-wktn), lot 4052011 (recorded 2019-02-07; mapped 2019-03-14; active); cross-checked against Pier 70 Design for Development Figure 6.4.2 - Building Height Maximum (p.155).

Assessor parcel

No document names the Assessor lot of Parcel E4. Lot 4052011 (active) was assigned to it from Pier & Point's reading of the plan diagram, so its outline is that lot.

Planning context

The D4D's land-use designation for E4 is Retail, Arts and Light Industrial (Figure 2.1.1), with office uses permitted in Building 12 and E4 except on the ground floor, and residential not permitted on E4 (Table 2.1.1) – not residential or general mixed-use as sometimes described secondhand. Port Item 12B (Feb 2020) calls it 'the arts building parcel (Parcel E4)' and lists it among the parcels excluded from the developer's standard option under the DDA. Recorded here as 'mixed' only because this map has no dedicated 'arts' use category. Max height 50 ft per Figure 6.4.2 – the lowest of any parcel at Pier 70. Port Item 11A (2026) proposes to allow residential use on E4 if an arts facility proves infeasible. No construction-start source found.

More detail (3)
  • Use rules for Parcel E4: the office and Priority Retail rules come from the D4D (updated April 26, 2022), which permits office uses in E4 except on the ground floor and requires Priority Retail on at least 33% of its east and south frontages (S2.2.3).3
  • Size of the arts facility: Port Item 9B, Attachment A (September 22, 2017) describes 'an up to 90,000-square-foot waterfront arts facility'; the Port's project page (undated) lists '90,000 sq. ft. arts facility & replacement studio space'; the Port's groundbreaking release (May 10, 2018) says 'waterfront arts facility'.6, 7, 9
  • Noonan artists' replacement space: Port Item 9B (September 22, 2017) says the permanent space 'will likely be located on the site of Parcel E4'; the Port's parameter rent was then $1.30/gsf.7
