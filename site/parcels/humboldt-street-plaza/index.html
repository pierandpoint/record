Humboldt Street Plaza

Park · Potrero Power Station

Also known as Humboldt Plaza (IMP name)

Planned small, mostly paved flexible-use plaza between Blocks 4 and 9 at the foot of Humboldt Street, giving a car-free connection from Delaware Street to the Blue Greenway and a waterside Bay overlook terrace (D4D 4.24, 4.19.2). A Phase 2 open space due before the first certificate of occupancy for the building representing 3 million sq ft of development; no design review or construction has been reported.3, 6, 2

Milestones and changes

  • 2031Status

    Humboldt Street Plaza: Completion expected: Humboldt Street Plaza is a Phase 2 open space under the revised Development Agreement phasing, keyed to a 3-million-sq-ft occupancy trigger rather than a calendar dateexpected, by phase schedule: Phase 2 in the October 2020 Phase 1 and Phasing Plan Amendment (revised from six phases to three over the same 16 years); SocketSite (Oct 9, 2020) reports Phase 2 running 2026-2031. The plaza's actual delivery trigger is the first certificate of occupancy for the building representing 3 million sq ft of total development, not a calendar date, so this is Phase 2's outer window, not a plaza-specific date.

    California Barrel Company / SF Planning (M-Files: 'Potrero Power Station Phase 1 and Phasing Plan Amendment Applications'), 2020-10; SocketSite, 2020-10-09

Open questions

Looked for, not found

  • No acreage for Humboldt Street Plaza is stated in the D4D, DEIR, IMP, DA recitals, Phase 1 application or annual reports; the geometry is a rectangle between Blocks 4 and 9 without a sourced area.
  • No design review application, cost, or construction date exists in any source; the plaza is a Phase 2 item behind a 3-million-sq-ft occupancy trigger that no source dates. The added expected-completion milestone therefore uses Phase 2's overall window (2026-2031, SocketSite Oct 2020), not a plaza-specific date.
  • The interim Prequel Park (Power Station sub-area polygon in the repo) shares edges with this plaza; whether the interim park occupies the plaza's footprint is not shown in any scaled drawing on file.
  • BOS File 260724 (Amended and Restated DA, 2026) amends the Phasing Plan; possible changes to this plaza's phase were not checked (draft DA not read).

About this shape

Accuracy

APPROXIMATE -- Figure 6.2.3 labels this open space directly, shown as a small plaza area between Block 4 (north) and Block 9 (south). This polygon is a simplified rectangular approximation of that labeled area, built directly from Block 4 and Block 9's own already-committed corners (see digitization_method) so it shares an exact edge with both -- its accuracy now tracks whatever those two files carry.

Traced from

Potrero Power Station Design for Development, Figure 6.2.3 - Building Height Plan (p.245).

Why there’s no DataSF parcel

Open space with no subdivided Assessor parcel of its own within the largely unaddressed former power-station site.

Planning context

A small public plaza per the D4D's own Figure 6.2.3 label, between Block 4 and Block 9 near the site's waterfront edge.
