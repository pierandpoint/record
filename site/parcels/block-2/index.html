Block 2

249 Craig Ln · Commercial · Potrero Power Station

Also known as UCSF Clinical and Life Sciences Building, Potrero Power Station Block 2

Herzog & de Meuron-designed building at 420 23rd Street / 249 Craig Ln, Block 2 of Potrero Power Station, for UCSF outpatient healthcare including a proton therapy center, laboratory space and ground-floor retail; construction started August 2025.1, 4

Open questions

Looked for, not found

  • The UC Regents' approval of the project as a Regents item (rather than only the September 2024 CEQA findings attachment found) was not independently located; the exact Regents meeting date/action is not confirmed beyond the CEQA findings document.
  • Reported square footage varies across four dated sources (Aug 2025 SF YIMBY, Feb 2026 SF YIMBY, the Feb 2026 Planning Director memo, and Bisnow) by as much as 15,000-40,000 sq ft; no single figure is treated as final here.
  • Overall completion date is reported inconsistently: Chronicle (Jul 2026) says opening in phases from 2028; Bisnow (May 2025) said completion by 2029; SF YIMBY (Feb 2026) says overall completion expected 2030. Not reconciled.
  • The Real Deal's May 9, 2025 article on the $575M bond issuance could not be fetched (paywalled/CAPTCHA); Bisnow's coverage of the same financing was used instead.
  • No source found giving a signed lease date between UCSF and the developer, or the term of that lease.

Research notes

Block 2, 249 Craig Ln -- Herzog & de Meuron-designed UCSF life sciences/cancer research building; foundation excavation underway Feb 2026, completion expected late 2028 (sfyimby.com/2026/02/excavation-work-underway-for-potrero-power-station-block-2-san-francisco.html). CORRECTED 2026-09-16: this row previously pointed to blklot 4232022 (151 Humboldt St), added on a text-match to this same article's mention of 'Block 2' without checking geometry. The D4D's Figure 6.2.3 places Block 2 west of Block 3, fronting Craig Lane -- blklot 4232022's real Assessor geometry instead sits ~250m south, roughly where the diagram's Block 8 is, with no visible construction activity there in satellite imagery. blklot 4175027 (active parcel, address 249 Craig Ln, recorded 2022-08-30) sits exactly where the diagram places Block 2, and its boundary precisely outlines a real excavation pit with visible crane/equipment in current satellite imagery -- a strong match to this article's 'excavation underway' description. The '151 Humboldt St' address from the original (wrong) row could not be independently reconfirmed against this corrected parcel and may itself have been an error; flagging rather than asserting it. blklot 4232022's own true identity is still unconfirmed and is NOT claimed to be any other specific numbered block here.
