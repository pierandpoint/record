Pier 70 Parcel E3

Entertainment · Pier 70

Also known as Elevation Sky Park San Francisco (interim use)

Waterfront residential parcel at 22nd Street and the Bay, 70 ft under the D4D and assigned to Phase 3A in the Port's proposed 2026 phasing plan. Its D4D building has no construction record; instead Elevation XR, with Brookfield, is opening the interim Elevation Sky Park entertainment venue (three projection domes and a pyramid on about 165,000 sq ft, a five-year term) on 'Phase 2/3 sites' here, with the first public event on September 19, 2026.5, 7, 6, 10, 1

Open questions

Looked for, not found

  • No construction start, building permit or vertical DDA for this parcel was found in any Port Commission document through the August 11, 2026 informational report, which says the developer 'has not exercised an option of any Phase 1 option parcels'.
  • Per-parcel gross square footage and unit counts are not published in the D4D (which sets height, stories and land-use scenarios, not parcel programs); the SUD's per-parcel programs would be in the SUD ordinance (Planning Code Sec. 249.79) or the Phase Submittal, neither of which was retrieved.
  • Placement of Elevation Sky Park on Parcel E3 is this project's own reading (merged into E3 by the owner's decision, per the geometry file): the primary sources say only 'Phase 2/3 sites' and 'where 22nd Street meets the Bay', which is consistent with E3/H2 but not parcel-specific. A Port license or permit for the venue, which would settle it, was not found on any 2025-2026 Port Commission agenda.
  • The opening date of September 19, 2026 is two days after this record's verification date and is recorded as expected; the Business Wire release itself could not be read (HTTP 403).
  • Lease or license terms (rent to Brookfield or the Port) for the interim venue: not found.

About this shape

Accuracy

APPROXIMATE -- this block's boundary is a hand-traced D4D diagram parcel, not a surveyed lot; treat vertex positions as accurate to roughly 5-15m, not survey-grade (see digitization_method for the transform's validation). EXTENDED SOUTH 2026-09-17 (user-flagged): this block's south edge fell ~22m short of the real sidewalk visible in satellite imagery, part of the same local scale error documented on Parcel A/E2 (parcels far from the transform's Illinois/Michigan St anchors land short of their true extent). Extended both south vertices by 22m to reach the sidewalk. Note this is still the D4D diagram's original 'E3' boundary, not a re-survey of ESP's own real tent/dome footprint -- see the ESP-merge note below for that separate uncertainty. CURRENT USE, POST-LEASE FUTURE UNCLEAR (added 2026-09-17): this parcel is being treated as the site of Elevation Sky Park, a real interim entertainment venue confirmed to be under a 5-year lease (see regulatory_context) -- that part is sourced fact. What is NOT known, and should not be assumed, is what happens here after that lease ends: the D4D's original masterplan designates this parcel 70ft-max residential, but that plan was adopted years before ESP's interim lease existed, and no source found ties ESP's lease expiration to an actual scheduled residential construction start, or confirms the D4D's original boundary/use for this specific parcel will still be pursued unchanged by then. Do not read the 'entertainment' use here as a permanent reclassification, or assume 'residential' will automatically resume -- both are open questions. The parcel-level match to ESP itself is also still an approximation: a rendering (SFGATE, Sept 2026) corroborated the general siting (shoreline-adjacent, near the historic warehouse core) but is an unscaled marketing image, not a site plan -- ESP's real footprint may still extend slightly into neighboring E2/E4, as the pre-merge overlap analysis found.

Traced from

Pier 70 Design for Development, Figure 6.4.2 - Maximum Building Height (p.155).

Why there’s no DataSF parcel

A real Assessor parcel may exist under this block (part of Brookfield's larger, largely unaddressed 28-acre site), but it has not been subdivided per the masterplan's individual named parcels -- this uses hand-traced approximate geometry instead.

Planning context

D4D-designated use: residential, 70 ft max per Figure 6.4.2 (no construction-start source ever found for that plan). CURRENT REAL USE: Elevation Sky Park (Elevation XR, in partnership with Brookfield Properties), a 165,000 sq ft interim entertainment venue (3 geodesic domes + a pyramid) under a 5-year lease, fully permitted through the Port of San Francisco, opened to the public September 19, 2026 ('Pier Play San Francisco'). Port materials describe it as an 'interim activation on Phase 2/3 sites within Pier 70 development zone' -- confirming it is temporary by design, though not tied to a specific successor project or date.
