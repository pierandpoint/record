Pier 70 Building 102 (former Power House)

Commercial · Pier 70

Also known as Building 102, Powerhouse, Power House No. 1, Compressor House

The 1912 Classical Revival powerhouse on 20th Street between Buildings 101 and 104, designed by Charles Peter Weeks, which generated compressed air and distributed electricity for the shipyard and still housed BAE Systems' electrical service when rehabilitation began. Orton rebuilt it as office space with a mezzanine and a rear addition (about 15,300-18,000 sq ft) and subleased it to JUUL Labs for ten years; the current occupant is unknown.2, 4, 7

About this shape

Accuracy

Real, surveyed building footprint -- high confidence in the outline itself. This footprint's area (~1955 m2 / ~21,043 sqft) is roughly double the '11,266 sf' figure this project's own earlier research attached to 'Building 102 (former Compressor House)' -- that figure was not independently re-verified this pass, and could describe interior/leasable area rather than the full building footprint, or could itself be imprecise. Flagging this discrepancy rather than silently picking one number; treat the footprint geometry (real, surveyed) as more reliable than the previously-cited sqft figure.

Traced from

DataSF Building Footprints dataset (real, surveyed building outline; not tied to an Assessor parcel).

Why there’s no DataSF parcel

This parcel (blklot 4046001) has no street address in DataSF's Assessor roll. The real Assessor lot groups 5 named buildings into one polygon with no further official subdivision -- this feature uses the real building footprint instead, extracted and matched individually.

Planning context

The former Power House, built 1912, part of the same 20th Street Historic Core rehabilitation by Orton Development Inc. (ODI) as Building 101 (HPC packet 2013.1168E, Nov 26 2013 review). Per that packet, Building 102 was 'in fairly good condition' at the time and in use as the powerhouse facility. No confirmed real-world tenant identified by any source found.
