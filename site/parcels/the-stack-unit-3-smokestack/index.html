The Stack (Unit 3 smokestack)

Entertainment · Potrero Power Station

Also known as The Stack, Boiler Stack, Unit 3 Boiler Stack

A 300-foot concrete boiler exhaust stack built in 1965 alongside the Unit 3 Power Block; explicitly retained as an existing structure under the Design for Development, and slated to anchor a temporary art installation within the interim Prequel Park.1, 2, 4

About this shape

Accuracy

APPROXIMATE -- Figure 6.2.3 marks this with a single point symbol and the label 'STACK / EXISTING STRUCTURE (~300')', not a footprint outline, but this polygon has been rebuilt 2026-09-16 (second pass) to trace the structure's actual visible footprint in satellite imagery (a fallen/lowered stack lying on the ground) rather than a diagram-derived placeholder. Treat as accurate to roughly 5-10m -- limited mainly by how precisely the lon/lat grid overlay could be read against the image, not by diagram-transform error.

Traced from

Potrero Power Station Design for Development, Figure 6.2.3 - Building Height Plan (p.245).

Why there’s no DataSF parcel

This is an existing structure without its own subdivided Assessor parcel -- it sits within the larger, largely unaddressed former power-station site.

Planning context

The historic ~300 ft tall smokestack from the power station's Unit 3 generating block, explicitly called out on the D4D's own Figure 6.2.3 as an 'Existing Structure' the plan retains (unlike the rest of the demolished power block) -- a literal, physical landmark left standing amid the redevelopment, sitting within Block 9's mapped development envelope (see potrero_block_9.geojson) at the site's waterfront edge. The Block 9 Building Configurations inset on the same figure shows two alternate massing options labeled 'WITH UNIT 3' and 'WITHOUT UNIT 3' -- suggesting the adjacent Unit 3 power block building (distinct from the Stack itself) may or may not be retained; the diagram does not draw Unit 3's own footprint distinctly enough to trace separately, so it is not mapped here as its own feature.
