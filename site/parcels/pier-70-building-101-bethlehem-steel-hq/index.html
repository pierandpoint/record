Pier 70 Building 101 (Bethlehem Steel HQ)

Commercial · Pier 70

Also known as Building 101, Bethlehem Steel Administration Building, 590 20th Street, RH San Francisco, The Gallery at the Historic Bethlehem Steel Building

The 1917 Classical Revival main office of the Union Iron Works at the corner of 20th and Illinois Streets, designed by Frederick H. Meyer, rehabilitated by Orton Development under the Port's 66-year Historic Core master lease and subleased whole to RH (Restoration Hardware) on a 41-year sublease from February 2017; RH San Francisco, with the Palm Court restaurant and a rooftop park, opened May 13, 2022.4, 6, 8

About this shape

Accuracy

Real, surveyed building footprint (DataSF Building Footprints) -- high confidence in the outline itself. The match to 'Building 101' specifically (as opposed to a neighboring structure) rests on position (33.9m from the diagram's own label, closest of any candidate) and shape correspondence, not an address or explicit building-number tag in the footprint dataset itself, since no such attribute exists there.

Traced from

DataSF Building Footprints dataset (real, surveyed building outline; not tied to an Assessor parcel).

Why there’s no DataSF parcel

This parcel (blklot 4046001) has no street address in DataSF's Assessor roll, and the building's own press addresses do not resolve to any DataSF parcel either. The real Assessor lot groups 5 named buildings (101, 102, 104, 122, 123) into one polygon with no further official subdivision -- this feature uses the real building footprint instead, extracted and matched individually.

Planning context

Bethlehem Steel HQ, a 1917 neoclassical office building designed by Frederick H. Meyer, ~80,000 sf, 5 floors plus 2 lower levels and a penthouse. Rehabbed by Orton Development Inc. (ODI) starting after a Nov 26 2013 Historic Preservation Commission review (sfplanning.org HPC packet 2013.1168E); per that packet, the seismic upgrade uses shear walls, and the rehab preserves the historic exterior elevations while adapting the interior. Completed in stages ~2017-2020. Now houses RH San Francisco -- 'The Gallery at the Historic Bethlehem Steel Building' (showroom, Palm Court restaurant, rooftop bar), opened May 2022.
