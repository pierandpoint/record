Sites/Pier 70

Pier 70 Building 101 (Bethlehem Steel HQ)

Commercial · Pier 70

Also known as Building 101, Bethlehem Steel Administration Building, 590 20th Street, RH San Francisco, The Gallery at the Historic Bethlehem Steel Building

Completesince May 13, 20227, 10Record as of Oct 20, 2026 · sources last verified Sep 29, 2026

The 1917 Classical Revival main office of the Union Iron Works at the corner of 20th and Illinois Streets, designed by Frederick H. Meyer, rehabilitated by Orton Development under the Port's 66-year Historic Core master lease and subleased whole to RH (Restoration Hardware) on a 41-year sublease from February 2017; RH announced the opening of RH San Francisco, with the Palm Court restaurant and a rooftop park, on May 13, 2022. The California Office of Historic Preservation's 2023 Governor's Historic Preservation Awards include the Historic Shipyard at Pier 70 Rehabilitation, which covers 'a half dozen historic buildings known as "20th Street Historic Buildings at Pier 70"'; the award text does not name individual buildings, so it does not say whether Building 101 is one of them.4, 5, 6, 7, 10

Looked for, not found

  • Sources disagree on the year Building 101 was built. The Port's 2019 memo gives 1917 in its Table 1 (Year Built) and the 2013 Historic Preservation Commission packet says the building "was built in 1917", as does the National Register nomination this record cites; the same Port memo's narrative says the building "was built in 1915". Pier & Point shows 1917, the figure in the other sources and in the memo's own table; the 1915 figure is unexplained.
  • The exact completion / certificate of occupancy date for Building 101 (the Port's 2019 memo anticipated 2020; RH opened May 2022) was not found.
  • Whether Buildings 122 and 123, the two outbuildings behind Building 104 that share the master lease, are on this parcel or the Building 104 parcel is not settled by any source; Pier & Point's records group them with the north-side lot.
  • No 'now' photograph with a stated license was found; RH's press images are the tenant's copyright and are linked, not hosted.
  • The Historic American Engineering Record (HAER) survey of the Union Iron Works was not located in the Library of Congress catalog; the NRHP nomination is used as the historic primary source.
  • RH San Francisco and its Palm Court restaurant have not been re-confirmed as operating since RH's May 13, 2022 opening release: RH's own pages and OpenTable could not be checked on 2026-09-29, and no other dated source that could be checked was found.
  • Architects of Building 101: STRUCTURE magazine names Marcy Wong Donn Logan Architects and Mark Hulbert, Preservation Architecture among a Design Team that 'collaborated to seismically retrofit Buildings 14, 101, 102, 104, 115 and 116'; it does not assign each firm a role on Building 101. RH's own design architect is listed separately as the tenant's design architect.
  • Floor area of Building 101, by source: 56,268 sq ft (NRHP); 61,311 before and 60,525 after rehabilitation, plus a 9,000 sq ft new restaurant building behind it (Port 2019 Table 1); RH describes ~80,000 sq ft over five floors.
  • Seismic scheme of Building 101: as proposed in the 2013 Historic Preservation Commission packet, interior partitions 'would be removed' for an open floor plan and 'reinforced concrete shear walls will improve seismic safety'; no source read confirms this as built.

About this shape

Accuracy

Real, surveyed building footprint (DataSF Building Footprints) – high confidence in the outline itself. The match to 'Building 101' specifically (as opposed to a neighboring structure) rests on position (33.9m from the diagram's own label, closest of any candidate) and shape correspondence, not an address or explicit building-number tag in the footprint dataset itself, since no such attribute exists there.

Traced from

DataSF Building Footprints dataset (real, surveyed building outline; not tied to an Assessor parcel).

Assessor parcel

The HPC packet of Nov 2013 lists Assessor's Block 4046, Lot 001 (parcel 4046001) among the lots holding its ten 20th Street Historic Buildings, without saying which building is on which lot; DataSF's footprint for this building carries that lot as its legacy id (mblr SF4046001). DataSF shows that lot retired on 2018-10-02 (DataSF Assessor parcels, acdm-wktn); the building's footprint now lies within active lot 3941027, which has no street address in DataSF's Assessor parcels. This feature uses the real building footprint instead, extracted and matched individually.

Planning context

Bethlehem Steel HQ, a 1917 neoclassical office building designed by Frederick H. Meyer. Its size is reported differently: RH's May 13, 2022 release calls it an '80,000-Square-Foot Landmark Building', the National Register nomination says it 'contains 56,268 square feet', and the Port gives 61,311 sq ft before and 60,525 sq ft after rehabilitation (HPC packet, Nov 2013; Port Item 12A, Jan 2019); no source settles the difference. Its floors are described differently too: the HPC packet says 'a five-story above street level office building' with 'two lower levels and a penthouse level', the nomination describes three full stories over a basement and sub-basement with a partial fourth floor and penthouse, and RH says 'five floors'. Rehabbed by Orton Development Inc. (ODI) starting after a Nov 26 2013 Historic Preservation Commission review (sfplanning.org HPC packet 2013.1168E); per that packet, the seismic upgrade uses shear walls, and the rehab preserves the historic exterior elevations while adapting the interior. The Port's Jan 2019 update reported Buildings 101 and 102 still under construction, with completion and occupancy of Building 101 anticipated in 2020. RH announced the opening of RH San Francisco – 'The Gallery at the Historic Bethlehem Steel Building' (showroom, Palm Court restaurant and two wine bars, rooftop park) in a press release dated May 13, 2022; the release gives no separate opening day, and no independent dated report was read.
