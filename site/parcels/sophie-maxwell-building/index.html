Sophie Maxwell Building

1212 Maryland St · Residential · Potrero Power Station

Also known as The Maxwell, Potrero Power Station Block 7B, 1212 Maryland St

Eight-story, 105-unit, 100% affordable workforce housing building at 1212 Maryland St (corner of Humboldt St), the first building completed in the Potrero Power Station redevelopment; designed by Leddy Maytum Stacy Architects, built by Swinerton for Found Middle LP (Associate Capital / Fifth Space), completed August 2025 and opened October 2025 for households at 50-110% AMI.6, 1, 5, 16, 24

Open questions

Looked for, not found

  • Bond amount and issuer for the CalHFA bond-recycling debt (and whether CSCDA or another conduit was involved): looked in the Port memo 10A, the four DA annual reports, the Chronicle (Oct 2023, Aug 2026), SF Standard (Oct 2025), SF YIMBY, Hoodline; no dollar amount for the bonds is stated anywhere.
  • Total development cost: only Hoodline (Dec 2025, citing the Chronicle) gives '$50 million'; the Standard gives $670,000 per unit (105 x 670,000 = about $70M). The two figures are inconsistent and neither is from a primary document.
  • Temporary Certificate of Occupancy / First Certificate of Occupancy date: the Port memo says construction was 'completed in August 2025'; no TCO date found. The 2025 DA annual report (due 2026) is not yet on the SF Planning page.
  • Construction start: the 2023 annual report and press give the groundbreaking ceremony as Oct 12, 2023 (site permit issued Oct 25, 2023), but the 2024 annual report says Found Middle 'commenced construction ... in June 2024' and the 2023 report says the construction contract 'commenced in 2024'. Both are recorded; the June 2024 date is probably the DA-defined commencement.
  • Design review approval date: the annual reports list Block 7B as 'Approved by Planning Department, 1.5.22; Planning Director Memo dated 3.2.22'. The signed approval letter is dated March 2, 2022; the Jan 5, 2022 event is not otherwise documented.
  • Parcel: the design review memo lists parent lots 4175/017, 4175/002, 4232/001 and 4232/006; the repo's blklot 4232020 must post-date the Phase 1 Final Map (recorded July 26, 2022). No document in the corpus states the new lot number; 'Lot 10' appears on the LMS plan sheets ('Block 7B - Lot 10').
  • Land conveyance to Found Middle: the DA Housing Plan requires conveyance at no cost in fee or by ground lease; the approved Affordable Housing Conveyance Agreement (March 19, 2024) is not in the corpus, so fee vs. ground lease and term are unknown.
  • Lottery: the SF Standard (Oct 15, 2025) says a random lottery would be held later that month, while the May 2025 Housing Plan amendment exempts 100% affordable projects that receive no direct City money from the MOHCD lottery. Whether Sophie Maxwell is a 'City Financed Unit' project is not stated.
  • Unit mix as built: Swinerton (contractor's own material) gives 70/21/14; the 2022 approval gives 63/28/7 of 98. No City document with the 105-unit mix was found.
  • Hoodline (Dec 6, 2025) calls it a 'six-story building'; every other source says eight stories. Treated as an error in Hoodline.
  • No hostable 'now' photograph found: Wikimedia Commons searches for 'Sophie Maxwell Building', 'Potrero Power Station' and 'Dogpatch Power Station' returned no photo of the building (only pre-2011 power plant images). Henrik Kam's photos (SF YIMBY, AIA CA) are not openly licensed; linked only.
  • Citizen Portal's AI-generated summary of the Oct 2025 opening mentions six Homeless Prenatal Program families among the first residents and 'resident parking'; not used because it is machine-generated from a video and the design approval states no vehicle parking.

What we searched for and could not source. A blank is better than a guess.
