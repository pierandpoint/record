Pier 70 Building 21 (Substation No. 5, relocated)

Mixed · Pier 70

Also known as Building 21, Substation No. 5, Electric Shop No. 2

A circa-1900 steel-framed, corrugated-metal-clad shop with a double-gable monitor roof (101 ft by 76 ft, 10,172 sq ft), the only surviving Risdon Iron Works building and the earliest steel-clad structure in the district, later Bethlehem's Substation No. 5. One of the three historic buildings the DDA obliges Brookfield to rehabilitate: a 66-year lease at $1 a year must be signed within a year of completing the building on Parcel E1 (Phase 2C), so no rehabilitation has begun.2, 5, 3

About this shape

Accuracy

REAL, surveyed building footprint (DataSF Building Footprints) as of 2026-09-17 -- high confidence in the outline itself and its match to Building 21: position matches multiple independent prose sources (adjacent to Building 12, fronting Slipways Commons) and its combined size (734.96 sq m) is within 4.4% of the previously-cited 101ft x 75ft (~703.7 sq m) documented footprint. The match rests on position, size, and satellite corroboration, not an explicit building-number tag in the footprint dataset itself (no such attribute exists there). A small 49 sq m footprint ~9m away was not included -- see digitization_method. Superseded history: STATUS CORRECTED 2026-09-13 -- this building's rehabilitation has not started as of the most recent primary sources found (see regulatory_context).

Traced from

DataSF Building Footprints dataset (real, surveyed building outlines; not tied to an Assessor parcel), cross-confirmed against ESRI World Imagery satellite tiles. SUPERSEDED 2026-09-17 a long chain of diagram/prose-based approximations (kept below for history) -- see digitization_method. Originally three converging real-world sources: (1) 2011 Port RFI "Pier 70 Preferred Master Plan" map (sfport.com/sites/default/files/FileCenter/Documents/939-Pier70_RFI_3-2-11_web.pdf, p.7/11), a scaled map with real street labels (Illinois St, 20th St) showing Building 21 immediately adjacent to Building 12 near the historic core, annotated "BUILDING 21 PROPOSED TO BE RELOCATED"; (2) Aug 3 2017 Port Commission memo (sfport.com/sites/default/files/Commission/Item%2011A%20Pier%2070%20Final.pdf, Exhibit F p.68): "Building 21 (Substation No. 5)... would be relocated approximately 75 feet to the southeast, and in its new location it would front Slipways Commons... sits directly next to the proposed new waterfront arts facility"; (3) D4D Figure 3.6.1 "Illustrative Slipways Commons Plan" (p.56/PDF p.72), a scaled plan with a real 0-50-100ft bar and "MARYLAND ST" label, captioned "the historic node of Buildings 2, 12, and 21."

Why there’s no DataSF parcel

This structure has no street address in DataSF's Assessor roll and sits on the same huge, largely-unsubdivided Assessor lot (blklot 4052001) as Building 14 and other structures across central Pier 70 -- no distinct blklot exists to join for Building 21 specifically, so this feature uses the real building footprint instead, matched individually (see digitization_method).

Planning context

Building 21, originally Substation No. 5, was relocated on-site and is one of three historic structures (with Buildings 2 and 12) the Pier 70 Design for Development commits to rehabilitating per the Secretary of the Interior's Standards (Pier 70 D4D S6.6.1). CORRECTED 2026-09-13: an earlier version of this row marked it "complete," inferred (not directly cited) from Building 21 being grouped in the D4D's rehabilitation commitment alongside Buildings 2 and 12. A follow-up research pass found the Port's own 28-acre site page (sfport.com/projects-programs/pier-70-28-acre-site) describes Building 21's rehabilitation as still to come -- the page shows a rendering, not a photo, captioned "Rendering of Pier 70 Building 21," under a "Rehabilitation of three historic buildings" heading, implying a future vision rather than a completed project. This is corroborated negatively by the Port's own June 2025 project-update deck (sfport.com/sites/default/files/2025-06/Pier%2070%20Update%20-%20June%202025%20v3.pdf), which documents Building 12 and Building 15's full rehabilitation histories in detail but never mentions Building 21 having been touched. Status corrected from complete to existing (see status.csv for this value's definition) -- a standing historic structure, not yet rehabbed, with no confirmed construction timeline.
