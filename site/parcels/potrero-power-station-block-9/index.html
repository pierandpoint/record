Potrero Power Station Block 9

Mixed · Potrero Power Station

Also known as Block 9, Unit 3 hotel site

Planned ~240,000-gsf, 250-room waterfront hotel site incorporating the retained Unit 3 Power Block; as of May 2025 the developer said the hotel 'could be a decade away' and proposed an interim park (Prequel Park) on the land instead.1, 3

About this shape

Accuracy

APPROXIMATE -- this block is not yet built and does not correspond to any subdivided Assessor parcel: it currently sits on vacant/graded land within the former power station site (unaddressed in DataSF's Assessor roll). Traced from an adopted regulatory diagram via multi-point real-street georeferencing (see digitization_method), but this diagram is dated 2020 and may not reflect later design changes (as already found for the UCSF/Sophie Maxwell area -- see digitization_method). Treat vertex positions as accurate to roughly 10-15m (rebuilt 2026-09-16 via the site's GDAL/TPS pass -- see digitization_method), and treat the block's applicability itself as somewhat more provisional than Mission Rock's or Pier 70's equivalent entries. Its overlap with the real, under-construction UCSF Block 2 area (a separate, better-sourced feature elsewhere in this dataset) has not been independently re-checked in this pass.

Traced from

Potrero Power Station Design for Development, Figure 6.2.3 - Building Height Plan (p.245).

Why there’s no DataSF parcel

A real Assessor parcel may exist under this block (part of the site's larger, largely unaddressed footprint), but it has not been subdivided per the masterplan's individual named blocks -- this uses hand-traced approximate geometry instead.

Planning context

Flexible commercial/hotel/residential per the D4D. Figure 6.2.3's own 'Block 9 Building Configurations' inset shows heights up to 177 ft max (in the 'with Unit 3' configuration) -- exceeding an 85-145 ft range sometimes cited secondhand. This block's real-world area overlaps where Unit 3 and the Stack physically stand (mapped separately, both real/existing) and where Prequel Park's approximate interim-park geometry sits (also mapped separately, and excluded here via clipping) -- treat this polygon as the block's outer development envelope, not a claim that all of it is buildable independent of those other real features.
