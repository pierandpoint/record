1215 Michigan St

Nearby proposal, outside Potrero Power Station

Data_center · Potrero Power Station

A 4-acre industrial parcel at 1215 Michigan St, currently home to crane-rental and heavy-hauling firm Sheedy Drayage, that the San Francisco Chronicle reported (Sept 2026) may become a roughly 160,000-square-foot data center. No application had been filed with SF Planning as of that report.1

About this shape

Accuracy

REAL Assessor parcel geometry (not approximate), but the local basemap (data/raw/parcels.geojson, buildings.geojson) was fetched to an older, slightly smaller bounding box that stops just north of this parcel, so the grey basemap land/building texture is blank immediately around this shape on the map -- cosmetic only; the parcel's own outline and position are exact. Fix: widen src/config.py's BBOX_SOUTH and re-fetch the raw layers.

Traced from

The real DataSF Assessor parcel for blklot 4241004 (1215 Michigan St, 4.07 acres, zoned PDR-1-G), fetched directly from the DataSF Socrata API (data.sfgov.org/resource/acdm-wktn.json, dataset 'Parcels - Active and Retired') on 2026-09-21. Routed through this table, not status.csv, only because the parcel sits just south of data/raw/parcels.geojson's cached bounding box (src/config.py's BBOX_SOUTH=37.7545; this parcel's northern edge is ~37.7542) and so isn't in that local extract -- the polygon itself is the real, unmodified Assessor shape, not hand-traced or approximated. A small blklot 4241003 (~0.25 acres) immediately west, also addressed 1215 Michigan St, is not included: the Chronicle's reporting and the Assessor's own lot_area (177,223 sq ft = 4.07 acres) both point to 4241004 as 'the' 4-acre parcel in question.
