Sites/Mission Rock/Verde

Verde

Residential · Mission Rock

Also known as Mission Rock Parcel F, Building F

Studio Gang's 23-story, 254-unit rental tower at 1070 Bridgeview Way, the fourth and last Phase 1 building. Construction started March 2022, leasing began April 2024, residents moved in from June 2024 and the ribbon was cut September 3, 2024; 59 of its units are below market rate after a 2024 re-mix toward 90% AMI.8, 16, 17, 9

Open questions

Looked for, not found

  • Construction cost: not stated anywhere read; only the $116.2M Wells Fargo loan is on record.
  • Exact TCO date: the Port projected May then June 2024; no document read confirms the issued date.
  • Topping-out date: the Port's October 2023 memo only says it had topped out; no dated press item found.
  • Total square footage disagrees across sources (287,000 / 314,508 / 315,000); a permit record would settle it.
  • Images: no 'before' photo is hosted (until 2020 the parcel was the Seawall Lot 337 surface parking lot) and no rendering is hosted because the architects' renderings are not openly licensed; they are reachable through the architect sources listed here.
  • A Mapillary storefront photo of Ike's Love & Sandwiches (a sourced retail tenant, see the retail fact above) was hosted briefly and then removed: on review it read as too cluttered (a trash bin, a parked car and several pedestrians in front of the sign) to keep.
