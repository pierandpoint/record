Potrero Power Station Block 7 (remaining portion)

Mixed · Potrero Power Station

Also known as Potrero Power Station Block 7A, Block 7 (remaining portion), Power Station Block 7A tower

The L-shaped southwestern portion of Block 7 at Humboldt St and Louisiana Paseo, approved March 2, 2022 for a Foster + Partners-designed 27-story, approximately 237-ft market-rate tower with 325 units, ground-floor retail and childcare over a basement garage; the tallest building permitted on the site. No building permit or construction start has been reported as of September 2026.1, 6, 8

Open questions

Looked for, not found

  • Construction start: none found. Searched the 2021-2024 DA compliance reports (the 2024 report says no market-rate building permits have been submitted), SF YIMBY through Sept 2026, the Chronicle (Jul 29 and Aug 3, 2026), SF Standard (Oct 2025) and Potrero View (Oct 2025). SocketSite (Jul 2021) said construction was 'slated to commence in 2023'; SF YIMBY (Mar 2021) said Block 7 construction was expected to start 'by 2023, with completion by 2026'. Neither happened.
  • Whether the July 2026 Chronicle line 'tallest residential tower would be permitted to reach 248 feet' refers to Block 7A: the D4D allows 240-ft highrise towers on both Block 5 and Block 7, and the amendment package also lets Block 5 flip between residential and commercial. Not confirmed for 7A specifically; the Planning Commission packet for July 30, 2026 was not in the corpus.
  • Unit count drift: 277 (press 2021) -> 325 (approval 2022) -> 319 (compliance reports' LBE appendix) -> 315 and '420' on Planning Commission slides (the 420 may be Block 7A+7B). Which figure a future permit will carry is unknown.
  • SF Standard (Oct 15, 2025) says Associate Capital 'will move on to constructing a 300-unit market-rate apartment building' next; the Aug 2026 Chronicle names Block 8 (342 units) as the next building, so 7A does not appear to be next.
  • Ground-floor childcare (6,000 GSF per DA Phasing Plan) is assigned to Block 7 in the compliance reports; the April 2023 childcare/HPP phasing amendment (docs/phasing_childcare_hpp.pdf) was not read for this record and may have moved it.
  • No cost, financing, general contractor or ground-lease information exists for Block 7A in any source found; the land is developer-owned (not Port), so no Port lease applies.
  • The 'Block 7A' / 'Block 7B' names are the sponsor's; the D4D treats Block 7 as one block with a single 240-ft highrise envelope. The repo's geometry note saying 7A/7B are 'informal press/developer labels' is consistent with the Planning documents, which nonetheless use 7A/7B as case names.

What we searched for and could not source. A blank is better than a guess.

About this shape

Accuracy

APPROXIMATE -- this block is not yet built and does not correspond to any subdivided Assessor parcel: it currently sits on vacant/graded land within the former power station site (unaddressed in DataSF's Assessor roll). Traced from an adopted regulatory diagram via multi-point real-street georeferencing (see digitization_method), but this diagram is dated 2020 and may not reflect later design changes (as already found for the UCSF/Sophie Maxwell area -- see digitization_method). Treat vertex positions as accurate to roughly 10-25m, not survey-grade, and treat the block's applicability itself as somewhat more provisional than Mission Rock's or Pier 70's equivalent entries. Rebuilt 2026-09-16 via GDAL GCP/TPS georeferencing -- see digitization_method. Re-measured 2026-09-16 (third pass) in a single consistent pixel-detection pass across the whole block layout -- see digitization_method. Re-corrected 2026-09-16 (fourth pass) after fixing a mis-measured GCP -- see digitization_method. CLIPPED 2026-09-17 (real-vs-approximate overlap audit, prompted by a user question about validation consistency): despite this file's own regulatory_context already stating it was 'clipped to exclude Sophie Maxwell's real footprint,' a from-scratch real-vs-approximate overlap check found this polygon still overlapped Sophie Maxwell (blklot 4232020) by 1,082 sq m -- the clip described in that text was apparently never actually applied to (or was lost from) the committed geometry. Re-clipped now via shapely difference() against blklot 4232020's real parcel boundary; area dropped from ~4,965 to ~3,883 sq m. This overlap had gone undetected because check_geometry_quality.py only ever checks approximate-vs-approximate sibling overlaps, never real-vs-approximate ones.

Traced from

Potrero Power Station Design for Development, Figure 6.2.3 - Building Height Plan (p.245).

Why there’s no DataSF parcel

A real Assessor parcel may exist under this block (part of the site's larger, largely unaddressed footprint), but it has not been subdivided per the masterplan's individual named blocks -- this uses hand-traced approximate geometry instead.

Planning context

Figure 6.2.3 shows Block 7 as ONE combined parcel (150 ft base / 240 ft max upper height on its east side, 150 ft max on its west side) -- 'Block 7A' and 'Block 7B' are informal press/developer labels for the two buildings actually being built on it, not the D4D's own numbering. The Sophie Maxwell Building (informally '7B', real blklot 4232020, already mapped separately in this dataset) occupies part of this block; this polygon is the REMAINING portion, clipped to exclude Sophie Maxwell's real footprint. The remainder is presumed to correspond to the informally-named 'Block 7A' (Foster + Partners-designed rental tower, real press-reported heights of 240-264 ft depending on source, closely matching this figure's 240 ft max) -- design review approved, no construction-start source found.
