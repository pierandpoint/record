Prequel Park (Port sub-area)

Park · Potrero Power Station

Also known as Port sub-area shoreline parcel, Portions of Seawall Lot 349 and Pier 72

Port-owned shoreline land (portions of Seawall Lot 349 and Pier 72; the DEIR's 1.6-acre 'Port sub-area' piece between the Power Station and the Bay, site of a potential recreational dock) leased to California Barrel Company for 66 years under Port Lease L-16662 for public open space. About 0.4 acre of the ~2-acre interim Prequel Park is to be built on it; the Port Commission amended the lease's permitted uses on Nov 18, 2025 (Resolution 25-70).9, 4, 10, 12

Open questions

Looked for, not found

  • BCDC amendment 2019.006.02: the repo's geometry file and data/milestones.csv say it was 'issued June 19, 2025'. The BCDC permit tracker gives June 19, 2025 as the date the application was RECEIVED and June 26, 2026 as the date ISSUED. The amendment document itself was not found on bcdc.ca.gov (2018-2019 DRB/Commission URLs now return 404; the tracker is an embedded table that could not be saved as text). Treat 2025-06-19 as application date, 2026-06-26 as issuance.
  • Port memo 10A is internally inconsistent: p.2 says construction start Q1 2026 / opening Q1 2027; p.4 says Q3 2026 / Q3 2027. Both are recorded; the p.4 dates follow the DBI-submittal sentence and are the more detailed statement.
  • No dated source confirms that Prequel Park construction has started as of 2026-09-17 (searched sfport.com, sfplanning.org, Chronicle, SF YIMBY, Potrero View, SF Standard, developer site, ICA SF). The geometry file marks the Power Station sub-area half 'under_construction' without a source.
  • The 'Spring 2026 SF development pipeline' listing ('Potrero Power Station - Prequel Park', developer FIFTH SPACE, scope demolition/grading/utilities/stormwater/landscaping) cited in the geometry file could not be located online; no URL is on file.
  • Lease commencement date and the recorded lease are not in the corpus; 'March 2087' implies a ~March 2021 start but no source states it.
  • Exact footprint of the ~0.4 acre on Port land: memo Exhibit 1 is an unscaled rendering; not digitized.
  • Whether the 2024 annual report's 'temporary activation east of Delaware Street and Stack Plaza' (Andrea Cochran Landscape Architecture, construction then expected Q1 2026) is the same project later branded Prequel Park with Heatherwick Studio is not stated by any source.
  • Planning temporary use authorization (June 2025): no case number or document found on sfplanning.org.
  • Port Commission minutes of Nov 18, 2025: item 10A passed on consent with no discussion or public comment; the minutes URL is https://www.sfport.com/media/10953/download?inline (the meeting page's 'm11182025.pdf' link returns 'page not found').
  • Area of the whole interim park differs by source: ~2 acres (Port memo), 2.5 acres (developer site), 2.75 acres (Chronicle, ICA SF), 'almost three' (Potrero View); BCDC scope 57,000-62,000 sq ft. Recorded separately; not reconciled.

About this shape

Accuracy

APPROXIMATE ONLY -- digitized from an illustrative planning figure, not a surveyed record, then clipped to the real Port jurisdiction boundary on its water-facing side and opened to remove a spike artifact (see digitization_method). The corrected centroid falls east of the real Sophie Maxwell and UCSF Block 2 parcels, consistent with a bay-side location, and area now closely matches the EIR's stated ~1.6 acres for this piece. Treat vertex positions as accurate to roughly tens of meters on the land-facing sides (still convex-hull-smoothed over the true shape there), but the water-facing edge is now a real boundary, not hand-traced. Do not use for anything requiring exact boundaries.

Traced from

Draft EIR Figure 2-2, "Project Site Sub-Areas and Ownership" (Potrero Power Station Mixed-Use Development Project Draft EIR, Case No. 2017-011878ENV, October 2018, p. 2-6), which shows the Port-owned "Port sub-area" (SF Port ownership, ~2.9 acres total in 3 non-contiguous pieces) at true scale with a north arrow and a 400-ft scale bar.

Why there’s no DataSF parcel

This land has no Assessor's Block/Lot number at all -- confirmed directly in the same Draft EIR, footnote 1, p. 2-5: "The Port sub-area, and City sub-area described below, are not assessed properties, and therefore do not have assigned Assessor's Block numbers." It therefore cannot appear in DataSF's Parcels - Active and Retired dataset and cannot be joined via status.csv/blklot.

Planning context

Prequel Park is the ~2-acre interim open-space project (BCDC Application 2019.006.02, a non-material amendment issued June 19, 2025; SF Port Commission Resolution 25-70, Nov 18, 2025, amending Lease L-16662 with California Barrel Company LLC) of which ~0.4 acres sits on this Port sub-area land near the future recreational dock. Construction targeted Q3 2026, public opening targeted Q3 2027 per the Port's Nov 14 2025 memo (Calendar Item 10A).
