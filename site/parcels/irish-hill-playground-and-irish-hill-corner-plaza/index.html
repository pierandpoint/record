Irish Hill Playground and Irish Hill Corner Plaza

Park · Pier 70

Also known as Irish Hill Playground, Irish Hill Corner Plaza, Irish Hill Park

The roughly two-acre courtyard park beside the Irish Hill remnant, bounded by Parcels C1, C2, PKS, HDY1/2 and HDY3 (D4D Section 3.12), with the smaller Irish Hill Corner Plaza at Illinois and 22nd Streets (Section 3.11) as its gateway. The 2017 DDA lets the City ask the Hoedown Yard developer to build 'Irish Hill Park' with special-tax proceeds; nothing has been built and its Hoedown Yard neighbors remain in PG&E ownership.1, 4

Open questions

  • How much of Irish Hill was quarried, when, and by whom is not stated in the retrieved primary sources beyond the NRHP nomination's site descriptions; the geometry file's 'quarried away in the early 20th century' summary is unsourced here.
  • This record combines two D4D zones (3.11 and 3.12) into one geometry, per the geometry file's caveat.
  • No basis for an expected-completion date was found: the 2017 DDA's Schedule of Performance ties Irish Hill Park's outside date to 1-1.5 years after the last temporary certificate of occupancy for HDY1 or HDY2 ('a Port or Hoedown Yard Vertical Developer obligation, at Port's discretion,' Port Commission Item 9B, Sept 22, 2017, Table A, p.8), but the Hoedown Yard itself is outside the 28-Acre Project and has no reported schedule (see the HDY1/2 and HDY3 records), so no window can be dated here.

Why there’s no DataSF parcel

Open space with no subdivided Assessor parcel of its own within Brookfield's largely unaddressed 28-acre site.

Planning context

Part of the Pier 70 D4D's Open Space Network (Section 3.4): Irish Hill Playground (Section 3.12, ~2.0 acres), described as an open space adjacent to the existing remnant of Irish Hill (Section 3.2) -- a small rock outcrop, the last visible remnant of a larger hill largely quarried away in the early 20th century for shipyard construction. Irish Hill Corner Plaza (Section 3.11) is a separate, smaller named zone at the southwest corner of this same open area, near the Illinois St/22nd St corner.

Corner plaza components
Entry plaza at least 40 ft wide along 22nd and Illinois and at least 2,000 sq ft (S3.11.1), with interpretive elements on Irish Hill's history, a seating terrace and a passage to the playground; plantings kept low to preserve the view of the remnant's peak1
Irish hill history
The remnant is what survived of the hill leveled for the shipyard: the 2014 NRHP nomination says Building 12 was constructed 'on newly leveled ground where most of Irish Hill once stood' and that Building 2's land 'was formerly occupied by a portion of Irish Hill'3
