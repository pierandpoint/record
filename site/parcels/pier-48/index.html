Sites/Mission Rock/Pier 48

Pier 48

Entertainment · Mission Rock

Also known as Pier 48 Sheds A, B and C

A 1928-30 reinforced-concrete finger pier with two Tudor/Gothic Revival transit sheds around an open 'valley', the southernmost contributor to the National Register-listed Port of San Francisco Embarcadero Historic District. The Mission Rock entitlements call for a Secretary of the Interior's Standards rehabilitation (261,000 gsf of production, restaurant, retail and exhibition uses); its planned anchor tenant Anchor Brewing closed in 2023, and today the pier is leased to the Giants' China Basin Ballpark Company for parking and events.7, 10, 1, 8, 6

Research notes

Pier 48 -- historic pier (two parallel steel-truss transit sheds, Shed A and Shed B, plus a connecting Shed C added 1937-38 at the bayward end; ~261,000-263,000 gsf total incl. bulkhead buildings, aprons, and 'the Valley' -- a 33,800 gsf open-air corridor between Sheds A and B, historically used for rail cars/cargo transfer on remnant Belt Line Railway spur tracks). Built 1924-1930 (bulkhead wharf completed July 17, 1929); a contributing resource to the Port's National Register-listed Embarcadero Historic District. IDENTIFICATION: this blklot has no street address in DataSF's Assessor roll, but its real, surveyed shape (including the visible gap between sheds matching 'the Valley') matches Pier 48's documented footprint and location exactly when overlaid on satellite imagery -- confirmed, not approximate. Currently managed by Giants Enterprises as an event/exhibition venue (concerts, conferences, corporate galas) and used for interim parking/construction staging; full historic rehabilitation (required to follow the Secretary of the Interior's Standards, entitled for light industrial/PDR/artisan food-beverage manufacturing/commercial event space/maritime retail) has NOT happened -- Pier 48 sits on an aging pile-supported deck over water (unlike the filled/raised Seawall Lot 337 next door) and needs substantial substructure pile repair and seismic work first. Anchor Brewing was originally slated as anchor tenant for a ~209,000-212,500 sf production brewery/taproom spanning Sheds A, B, and the Valley; Anchor closed in 2023 and its 2024 buyer confirmed it will not reopen an SF brewery, so that plan is dead. Recorded as 'existing' (a real, actively-used historic structure, not yet redeveloped per the masterplan) rather than 'complete' or 'planned'. LOT CORRECTED 2026-09-17: this row joined blklot 9900048, which DataSF's Assessor roll retired on 2020-06-12 (date_rec_drop) when Seawall Lot 337 was subdivided; the live lot for the pier is 8719A001 (recorded the same day, active), whose 27,493 m2 also takes in the pier aprons -- the Design Controls' 'Pier 48 Apron' open space (Section 3.6) is therefore inside this record rather than drawn separately. The retired lot's shape had IoU 0.86 with the live one and both contain DataSF's 16,913 m2 Pier 48 building footprint (sf16_bldgid 201006.0000016).

5.84 acres (HRE); 261,700 sq ft / 6.01 acres in the Design for Development parcel table7, 2
Dimensions
Pier substructure 369 ft wide, 610 to 635 ft long; bulkhead wharf 53 ft by 500 ft; a planned 1,200-ft pier was shortened to 623 ft because of a mud-filled 'valley' 800 ft offshore7
Layout
Two parallel transit sheds (A and B) on either side of an open-air 'valley' with remnant Belt Line Railway spur tracks, joined at the outer end by Shed C; berthing on three sides7
Entitled rehabilitation
261,000 gsf of light industrial, restaurant, retail and exhibition uses under the Secretary of the Interior's Standards (HRE); the EIR project table gives 242,500 gsf7, 4
Permitted uses
Production and 'other' uses only under the Design Controls permitted land use table; residential, commercial, retail and parking-garage uses not permitted3
Current use
Interim parking and special events under a 10-year lease to China Basin Ballpark Company, LLC (Giants affiliate); marketed by Giants Enterprises as a 200,000+ sq ft venue for up to 10,000 people, 24 Willie Mays Plaza1, 6, 9
Interim parking
750 to 1,000 spaces, pending demand and valet operations (2019 Phase 1 submittal)9
