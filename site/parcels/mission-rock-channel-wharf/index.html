About this shape

Accuracy

APPROXIMATE but high confidence: all four edges are recorded DataSF lot lines (the notch of lot 8719A003), agreeing with the Design Controls figures to within a few metres. Planned, unbuilt open space; the notch is a working wharf / staging area today.

Traced from

DataSF Assessor lot lines: the notch of the Terry A Francois Boulevard lot 8719A003 east of the roadway, between the Pier 48 lot 8719A001 and Pier 50; cross-checked against Mission Rock Design Controls Figure 1.1 (p.10), Figure 3.1.1 (p.45) and Section 3.5 (p.68).

Why there’s no DataSF parcel

The wharf is part of the Terry A Francois Boulevard right-of-way lot 8719A003, which also carries the roadway and its paseo; joining status.csv to that lot would paint the whole boulevard.

Planning context

Dedicated open space, Design Controls Section 3.5: a paved working-wharf plaza with public access, seating, a large-scale industrial feature, consistent with Public Trust uses; reached from Channel Lane across TAFB.

Research notes

Planned waterfront plaza between Piers 48 and 50, not yet built.
