Pier 70 Parcel H1

Commercial · Pier 70

Commercial-office parcel on the south side of 22nd Street east of Maryland Street (west half of the H1/H2 pair), 90 ft and six stories under the D4D, assigned to Phase 3A with Parcel E3 in the Port's proposed 2026 phasing plan. No design, appraisal or construction record exists for it.3, 4

Open questions

Looked for, not found

  • No construction start, building permit or vertical DDA for this parcel was found in any Port Commission document through the August 11, 2026 informational report, which says the developer 'has not exercised an option of any Phase 1 option parcels'.
  • Per-parcel gross square footage and unit counts are not published in the D4D (which sets height, stories and land-use scenarios, not parcel programs); the SUD's per-parcel programs would be in the SUD ordinance (Planning Code Sec. 249.79) or the Phase Submittal, neither of which was retrieved.
  • Brookfield's leasing brochure reportedly gives a combined '+/- 430,000 RSF' for H1-H2 (recorded in the geometry file); not retrievable in this pass.
  • Whether the Elevation Sky Park interim venue extends onto H2 (the Port described it as 'where 22nd Street meets the Bay' on 'Phase 2/3 sites'): unresolved; see the Parcel E3 record.

About this shape

Accuracy

APPROXIMATE -- this block is not yet built and does not correspond to any subdivided Assessor parcel: it currently sits on vacant/graded land, surface parking, or an existing industrial building slated for demolition within Brookfield's 28-acre district (unaddressed in DataSF's Assessor roll). Redone 2026-09-14 with a corrected, shear-free transform (see digitization_method) -- treat vertex positions as accurate to roughly 5-15m, not survey-grade. NOTE: this polygon is NOT clipped against real streets or existing buildings -- unlike Potrero's approximate blocks, several of Pier 70's D4D blocks are sited on existing industrial buildings that will be demolished, so overlap with a currently-real building is often expected, not an error; a boundary that appears to touch or slightly cross a street centerline reflects this transform's 1-8m residual error, not a deliberate encroachment. Street-facing edge re-clipped 2026-09-17 against official curb-referenced street data -- see digitization_method.

Traced from

Pier 70 Design for Development, Figure 6.4.2 - Maximum Building Height (p.155).

Why there’s no DataSF parcel

A real Assessor parcel may exist under this block (part of Brookfield's larger, largely unaddressed 28-acre site), but it has not been subdivided per the masterplan's individual named parcels -- this uses hand-traced approximate geometry instead.

Planning context

Office (pure office, not mixed-use, per Brookfield's own leasing brochure), 90 ft max per Figure 6.4.2 (6-story cap per D4D Section 6.4.2, not the sometimes-cited '6-8 stories'). Brookfield's brochure gives a combined H1-H2 total of '+/- 430,000 RSF'. No construction-start source found.
