PKN Frontage Open Space (20th Street)

Park · Pier 70

Also known as 20th Street Plaza

The roughly half-acre '20th Street Plaza' of the D4D open-space network (Section 3.10), the landscaped entry plaza on Parcel K North's 20th Street frontage at Illinois Street, designed as the gateway from Dogpatch to the Historic Core with an entry plaza, seating terrace and planted garden. The Port's Parcel K North project lists a 20th St/Illinois St plaza among the buyer's obligations; it has not been built.1, 4

About this shape

Accuracy

LOW-MODERATE CONFIDENCE -- Figure 3.3.1 is an illustrative, non-dimensioned rendering (no scale-accurate internal boundaries, unlike the D4D's own numbered-parcel figures), so this is a simplified bounding-box trace, not a precise outline. No specific proper name was found for this feature in the D4D text found so far (unlike Irish Hill Remnant, Waterfront Terrace, Slipways Commons, and the Waterfront Promenade, which are each the subject of their own named, detailed sub-figure elsewhere in the document) -- treat this name as a neutral, location-based placeholder pending a better source, not a confirmed D4D name. Its south/east/west edges match PKN's newly-retraced boundary (see digitization_method); its north edge is now ground-truthed against the real 20th St centerline (6.8m curb offset) rather than Figure 3.3.1's own illustrative depth, which turned out to extend past the real street entirely. Resulting strip is real but thin (~305 sq m) -- narrower than the diagram's own illustrative rendering implies.

Why there’s no DataSF parcel

Open space with no subdivided Assessor parcel of its own within Brookfield's largely unaddressed 28-acre site.

Planning context

Part of the Pier 70 D4D's Open Space Network (Section 3), shown on Figure 3.3.1 as landscaped open space along Parcel K North's 20th Street frontage. No further detail (name, area, specific program) found in the D4D text located so far.
