Pier 70 Waterfront Open Space (Waterfront Terrace / Slipways Commons / Waterfront Promenade / Pier 70 Shoreline)

Park · Pier 70

Also known as Pier 70 Waterfront Open Space, Waterfront Terrace, Slipways Commons, Waterfront Promenade, Pier 70 Shoreline

The roughly five-acre waterfront park system of the Pier 70 D4D (Waterfront Terrace, Slipways Commons and Waterfront Promenade, Sections 3.5-3.7) plus the roughly 1,300-foot Pier 70 Shoreline (Section 3.8), to be built by the developer and owned by the Port. Phase 1 includes three acres of parks, but the DDA ties park completion to adjacent buildings and the Port's 2026 report says the Phase 1 parks will be built 'on a schedule that coincides with the delivery of adjacent vertical development', so none has been constructed.1, 2, 4

Open questions

Looked for, not found

  • No Port Commission schematic-design approval for any of the four waterfront open spaces was found on 2018-2026 agendas, so none appears to have reached that stage.
  • Which named spaces make up the three Phase 1 acres is not stated; the reading of Exhibit A above (Slipways Commons area in Phase 1) is this project's interpretation of a diagram.
  • Cost of the parks: the 2017 memo gives $260.5 million of eligible horizontal costs for all three phases but no park line item was extracted; the Phase 1 infrastructure Notice of Completion (Nov 2022) was described as a $150 million investment without a park breakdown.
  • Interim use: the Elevation Sky Park venue occupies waterfront land near 22nd Street for up to five years; whether it sits on future park land or only on parcels E3/H2 is unresolved (see the E3 record).

About this shape

Accuracy

APPROXIMATE, AND KNOWINGLY UNSUBDIVIDED -- combines four D4D-named open spaces (Waterfront Terrace, Slipways Commons, Waterfront Promenade, Pier 70 Shoreline) into one polygon because no source found draws a boundary between them; do not treat this as a single named place. Total traced area (~4.0-4.3 acres before/after clipping) is a reasonable match to the D4D's own combined figures (a ~5.0-acre waterfront area covering the first three, plus part of a ~1,300-linear-foot shoreline) but is not a precise subdivision or survey. Programmatically traced from a diagram's fill color, not hand-verified vertex-by-vertex.

Traced from

Pier 70 Design for Development, Figure 6.4.2 - Maximum Building Height (p.155); named/numbered per Figure 3.4.1 - Overview of Public Spaces (p.52).

Why there’s no DataSF parcel

Open space with no subdivided Assessor parcel of its own within Brookfield's largely unaddressed 28-acre site.

Planning context

Part of the Pier 70 D4D's Open Space Network (Section 3) -- specifically Sections 3.5 (Waterfront Terrace), 3.6 (Slipways Commons), 3.7 (Waterfront Promenade), and 3.8 (Pier 70 Shoreline, ~1,300 linear feet). Together with Building 12 Plaza/Market Square, 20th Street Plaza, Irish Hill Corner Plaza, and Irish Hill Playground, these comprise the D4D's total nine acres of public open space (Section 3.4).

Research notes

Newly added 2026-09-16 as part of a systematic pass adding this site's previously-untracked open space -- see accuracy_caveat for why this combines four named D4D spaces into one polygon.
