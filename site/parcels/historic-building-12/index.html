Sites/Pier 70/Historic Building 12

Historic Building 12

1070 Maryland St · Mixed · Pier 70

Also known as Building 12, Plate Shop No. 2, 1070 Maryland Street

The 1941 Navy-built plate shop and mold loft at the heart of the World War II 'New Yard' (248 ft square, Aiken monitor roof), the first and so far only Phase 1 building delivered under Brookfield's Pier 70 DDA: lifted about ten feet against sea-level rise (12 ft per the contractor), rebuilt by Perkins&Will (architect) and Plant Construction (contractor) as a ground-floor market hall, second-floor maker studios and third-floor offices, and issued a temporary certificate of occupancy in January 2022. The DDA terms call for a 66-year, $1-a-year Port lease (no executed lease has been found); nearly 90% leased by August 2026, with two venture capital firms leasing the whole third floor (The Real Deal reports General Catalyst signed for it).2, 11, 7, 8, 17, 3, 6, 1

Research notes

Historic Building 12, 1070 Maryland St -- the ground floor plan in the Building 12 leasing brochure (pier70sf.com) is labelled with Louisiana St, Maryland St, 22nd St and Potrero Point Plaza; seismic retrofit/adaptive reuse complete, hosting events, and a 2026-reported VC office lease (General Catalyst, 70,000 sqft, 3rd floor). CORRECTED 2026-09-13: previously joined to blklot 4045017 (DataSF Assessor parcels list it at 700 Illinois St), the wrong parcel (indicated by a user-supplied screenshot showing this project's Elevation Sky Park box, anchored on the old position, sitting on top of the real Crane Cove Park; satellite imagery then showed the old Building-12 point in an ordinary residential block, and the correct blklot 4114010 exactly outlines the real sawtooth-roofed industrial building visible in satellite imagery at 1070 Maryland St). An earlier version of this row also credited the RH showroom to this building -- that's actually Building 101, the separate 1917 Bethlehem Steel administrative building at the corner of 20th/Illinois St (sfchronicle.com/sf/article/pier-70-building-12-19513305.php, therealdeal.com/san-francisco/2026/01/27/vc-firm-joins-pier-70-in-significant-office-lease, en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Building_101_(San_Francisco), pier70sf.com/leasing/building-12/, pier70sf.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Building-12_Digital.pdf)
