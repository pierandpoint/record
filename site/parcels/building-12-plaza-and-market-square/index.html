Building 12 Plaza and Market Square

Park · Pier 70

Also known as Market Square, Building 12 Entry Plaza

The roughly 1.5-acre 'social centerpiece' of the Pier 70 open-space network (D4D Section 3.9): Market Square between Buildings 2 and 12 and Parcel D, the Building 12 entry plaza on 22nd Street, a Maryland Street platform for vendors and food trucks, and possibly the retained structural frame of Building 15. Building 12 itself reopened in 2022 and Maryland Street's paving is done, but the plaza is a Phase 1 park still tied to adjacent vertical development.1, 4, 2

About this shape

Accuracy

APPROXIMATE -- derived from a real, street-polygonized city block (not a diagram pixel-trace), subtracting Building 2, Parcel D, and Building 12's own committed geometries. This is a deliberate departure from pure diagram-tracing: since Figure 6.4.2's own green fill didn't precisely reach the real (satellite-corrected) edges of Building 2 and Building 12, using the real block boundary instead better matches both the drawing's evident intent (this block reads as fully built-out, buildings plus plaza, no gaps) and the satellite ground truth. The block polygon's north edge stops slightly short of Parcel D's own north edge (~14m, since the not-yet-built '21st St (NEW)' isn't in real street data) -- unioned with D's own extent to avoid an artificial gap there. Area (~1.38 acres) is close to, but still a bit under, the D4D's cited ~1.5 acres for this named space -- the remaining gap most likely reflects the plaza's true extent continuing south past the mid-block passage (labeled '15' on the diagram) into the block south of Building 12, which is not part of this feature.

Traced from

Pier 70 Design for Development, Figure 6.4.2 - Maximum Building Height (p.155); named per Figure 3.4.1 - Overview of Public Spaces (p.52), zone 5.

Why there’s no DataSF parcel

Open space with no subdivided Assessor parcel of its own within Brookfield's largely unaddressed 28-acre site.

Planning context

Part of the Pier 70 D4D's Open Space Network (Section 3.9), approximately 1.5 acres, wrapping the historic Building 12 (and Building 2) on their west and south sides between Louisiana St and Maryland St.
