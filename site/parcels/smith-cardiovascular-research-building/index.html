About this shape

Accuracy

REAL -- surveyed OpenStreetMap building footprint, not a hand trace or plan estimate. This is the opposite situation from Mission Bay's condo-mapped residential towers (Avalon, Radiance, etc.): here, ONE large Assessor land parcel spans SEVERAL UCSF buildings (confirmed against data/raw/parcels.geojson and DataSF's Assessor tax roll -- e.g. blklot 8711507 at 675 Nelson Rising Lane alone is ~13 acres, far larger than any single building on it), so using the parcel boundary per building would make multiple records overlap and double-count the same ground. The real building footprint is the only non-overlapping, honest shape for each one.

Research notes

~236,000 sq ft, five stories, ~$254 million project cost; headquarters of the UCSF Cardiovascular Research Institute, an L-shaped building with wet labs, an outpatient clinic and a vivarium. Architect SmithGroup with Jim Jennings Architecture (landscape by Andrea Cochran Landscape Architecture). Opened 2010 (year only; a specific Nov 1, 2010 date found in one search summary was not traceable to a primary document, so not used).
