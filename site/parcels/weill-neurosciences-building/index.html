Weill Neurosciences Building

Institutional · Mission Bay

Also known as UCSF Neurosurgery at San Francisco Mission Bay, Joan and Sanford I. Weill Neurosciences Building

Completesince Jul 20211, 2Record as of Oct 23, 2026 · sources last verified Sep 21, 2026

This record's geometry is a footprint identified by Pier & Point, west of and adjacent to Third Street Garage on OCII plan Block 23A; that address and adjacency match the Joan and Sanford I. Weill Neurosciences Building at 1651 4th Street, a six-story, roughly 282,000-283,000-sq-ft neuroscience research and clinical building (design architect Mark Cavagnero Associates with SmithGroup as architect of record) that opened in July 2021. UCSF Health's own 'Neurosurgery Mission Bay - Fourth St.' clinic listing gives the same 1651 4th Street address.2, 3, 4, 5

Looked for, not found

  • Pier & Point first identified the building here as UCSF Neurosurgery at Mission Bay from its own knowledge (correcting an earlier mis-resolution to Genentech Hall), and at first that identification was not independently corroborated by a primary source. Later research found strong, independent corroboration: a contractor project page names this same OCII Block 23A as the site of the Joan and Sanford I. Weill Neurosciences Building, and UCSF Health's own clinic-location page places its 'Neurosurgery Mission Bay - Fourth St.' service at 1651 4th Street, the Weill building's address, directly adjacent to Third Street Garage as this record's geometry describes. Not treated as fully closed: no single source explicitly ties the parcel-level footprint to the 1651 4th Street address by lot number.
  • Exact opening day is not confirmed: UCSF Health's Weill page gives only the month ('It opened its doors in July 2021'), which is the date shown. UCSF's own news article, dated 2021-10 by its web address, could not be read (access refused on 2026-10-12), so no source read gives an opening day.
  • Construction cost is not stated in any source read: Architectural Record's project data lists 'Cost: Withheld'. The only figure on record is the $357.6 million project budget approved by the UC Regents in May 2017 (Regents item F4: 'The total project cost of $357.6 million, including $12 million of capitalized interest incurred during construction'), a budget rather than a construction cost.
  • The March 2016 Regents preliminary-plans item (270,000 gsf, six stories) predates the Weill naming gift and 'Weill Neurosciences Building' name; identification with this record rests on the Block 23A site match (confirmed elsewhere in this record's own sources), not a name match in the 2016 item itself.
  • The opening month shown is July 2021, as UCSF Health's own Weill page says ('It opened its doors in July 2021'); the UCSF news release dated 2021-10 could not be read and is likely an announcement date. The March 2016 Regents item planned full budget approval for March 2017, while the Regents' budget and design item for this building (Item F4, cited in this record's sources) is for the May 17, 2017 meeting, so that schedule slipped. The March 2016 item is an agenda item requesting approval; the minutes recording its vote, and the minutes of the May 2017 meeting, were not read.

What we searched for and could not source. A blank is better than a guess.

About this shape

Accuracy

APPROXIMATE – an idealized rectangle (drawn by Pier & Point from the leftover open ground between official street lines and neighbouring mapped shapes, cut and aligned to Third Street Garage's own real orientation), not a hand-traced or surveyed footprint. The building name (Weill Neurosciences Building) is confirmed by UCSF's own campus map; UCSF Neurosurgery's presence in it, first based on Pier & Point's own knowledge, is corroborated by UCSF Health's own 'Neurosurgery Mission Bay - Fourth St.' location page, which gives 1651 4th Street, the same address as UCSF Health's Weill Neurosciences Building page.

Traced from

Drawn by Pier & Point, not a hand trace: one of the leftover pieces of open ground between DataSF's official street centerlines and neighbouring mapped shapes in this area (about 11,626 sq m before shaping), chosen by hand from a numbered map of those pieces, idealized to a proper rectangle sharing Third Street Garage's own west edge and orientation, with the north walkway strip (a narrow pinch point, width dropping to ~2m) cut off.

How this record was put together

OCII plan block 23A on UCSF's own campus map; a CEQAnet notice of determination (SCH 2013092047, Aug 4, 2017) calls it 'the planned Weill Neurosciences Building (Block 23A)'. Previously mismapped to Genentech Hall (a different, separate building further west); Genentech Hall's own real plan-block correlation is now an open question pending future review. UCSF Health lists 'Neurosurgery Mission Bay - Fourth St.' at 1651 4th Street, the address of the Weill Neurosciences Building, so UCSF Neurosurgery practices in this building; the building may house other departments as well.
