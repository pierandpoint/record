Waterfront Park

Park · Potrero Power Station

Also known as Waterfront Open Spaces

Legal deadline (not a schedule) · not an expected completion

Pier & Point is an independent, cited record of waterfront development. How we work →

Planned shoreline park along the site's Bay frontage: the D4D's ~3.6-acre 'Waterfront Open Spaces' (Waterfront Park with the Blue Greenway, The Point, Stack Plaza, Block 9 plazas and Humboldt Street Plaza), which SF Planning summarizes as a ~3-acre waterfront park opening over 1,000 linear feet of shoreline. About 2 acres of the project's open space is on Port-owned shoreline property leased under L-16662 (66 years, $1/year), with BCDC permit 2019.006 issued July 2021. Delivery is phased (Waterfront Park South before the certificate of occupancy for 3 million sq ft; The Point before 500,000 sq ft; North/West with Block 4); The Point's design review has been pending since Dec 2021, and the interim Prequel Park precedes the permanent park.2, 5, 10, 11, 16

Only the tracker row for amendment .02 was found.
  • No design review approval for The Point was found after the Dec 3, 2021 submittal; every annual report through March 2025 says 'Under Review'. The earlier expected-completion milestone (2031, from SocketSite's Oct 2020 Phase 2 window) was replaced on 2026-09-29 because that 2020 forecast has not held and the Port's Nov 2025 memo puts open-space delivery behind vertical development expected over the next 10-15 years; no park-specific or Point-specific date exists.
  • Seawall/shoreline construction: the only statement is Landa's 'building the seawall a few years ago' (Potrero View, Oct 2025); the Phase 1 horizontal work (pile driving, deep soil mixing) was substantially complete May 2023 but the annual report does not itemize shoreline protection.
  • Area: 3.6 acres (D4D, DA, DEIR) vs ~3 acres and '1,000 linear feet' (SF Planning page only) vs 4.0 acres (IMP); this record's map shape traces only the visible strip. Not reconciled.
  • This record's map shape overlaps the newly added Prequel Park (Power Station sub-area); the sources agree the interim park sits within the future waterfront open space, but no drawing on file shows the interim footprint at scale.
  • No construction cost or delivery date for the permanent park; the 3-million-sq-ft trigger is not dated by any source.
  • Port Commission Feb 25, 2020 minutes: the copy checked is the agenda text; the adoption of Resolution 20-12 is taken from the Nov 2025 memo's recital.
  • Whether BCDC is listed as a reviewing agency for the EIR on CEQAnet (State Clearinghouse 2017112005) was not re-checked as of 2026-09-29; only BCDC's scoping comment in the Draft EIR is cited.
  • Since 2026-10-11 The Point, Stack Plaza and Prequel Park (interim) have their own records (Prequel Park is now one record), so the shape on this record is only the remainder of the shoreline strip (about 0.18 acre) and is much smaller than the ~3-acre waterfront park SF Planning describes. About this shape

Accuracy

APPROXIMATE -- what is left of the shoreline strip once Prequel Park (interim), The Point and Stack Plaza have their own shapes: two pieces, one south of Block 9 beside Stack Plaza and one north of Block 4, together 740 m2 (0.18 acre). The Design for Development's Waterfront Park is much larger (the SF Planning project page lists 'The ~3-acre waterfront park'; Figure 4.19.1, printed p.99, draws a Waterfront Park Boundary along the shoreline strip it shows), so this shape is a remainder, not the park. It was formed by merging the earlier Waterfront Park trace with both Prequel Park sub-area drawings and cutting the result at Block 9's south edge and Block 4's north edge. The two pieces are the parts of the strip outside Prequel Park (interim)'s footprint: the permanent waterfront park is read to be built along the shoreline of the interim Prequel Park when that park ends, so that shoreline is Waterfront Park's future ground and is not drawn twice here. Same 15-25 m positional tolerance as this site's other entries.

Traced from

Potrero Power Station Design for Development, Figure 6.2.3 - Building Height Plan (p.245).

Assessor parcel

Open space with no subdivided Assessor parcel of its own within the largely unaddressed former power-station site. (This reflects Pier & Point's own parcel search, not something the cited Design for Development can establish; the D4D does not address Assessor parcels.) The Port shoreline premises are Port land under Lease L-16662 (Port Commission memo, Nov 14, 2025); DataSF Parcels (queried 2026-09-29) lists active lots in Assessor blocks 4175 and 4232 added in December 2023 after the Phase 1 Final Map, and whether any of them matches this park was not checked.

Planning context

The site's shoreline public-access open space, per the D4D's own Figure 6.2.3 label, running along the bay side of Blocks 9 and the Stack, connecting toward Warm Water Cove Park to the south (shown on the same figure, outside the project site boundary).

How this record was put together

Simplified partial trace of a long shoreline open space -- see the accuracy caveat; does not capture the strip's full north-south extent. Since 2026-10-11 The Point, Stack Plaza and Prequel Park (interim) are separate records and this record's shape is only the remainder of the shoreline strip.
