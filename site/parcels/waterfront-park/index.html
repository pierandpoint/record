Sites/Potrero Station/Waterfront Park

Waterfront Park

Park · Potrero Power Station

Also known as Waterfront Open Spaces, The Point (southern end), Stack Plaza (within the waterfront open spaces)

Planned shoreline park along the site's Bay frontage: the D4D's ~3.6-acre 'Waterfront Open Spaces' (Waterfront Park with the Blue Greenway, The Point, Stack Plaza, Block 9 plazas and Humboldt Street Plaza), which SF Planning summarizes as a ~3-acre waterfront park opening over 1,000 linear feet of shoreline. Largely on Port land leased under L-16662 (66 years, $1/year), with BCDC permit 2019.006 issued July 2021. Delivery is phased (Waterfront Park South before the certificate of occupancy for 3 million sq ft; The Point before 500,000 sq ft; North/West with Block 4); The Point's design review has been pending since Dec 2021, and the interim Prequel Park precedes the permanent park.5, 16, 11, 2, 10

Open questions

Looked for, not found

  • The brief's note that the BCDC permit application 2019.006.00 was 'filed complete June 2, 2020 per ceqanet' could not be verified: the CEQAnet entries (2017112005/2 and /3) show only the DEIR receipt (10/3/2018) and Final EIR receipt (12/11/2019) with BCDC as a reviewing agency. The original BCDC permit document (issued July 2021 per the Port memo) was not found on bcdc.ca.gov; the 2018-2019 DRB and Commission pages now return 404. Only the tracker row for amendment .02 was found.
  • No design review approval for The Point was found after the Dec 3, 2021 submittal; every annual report through March 2025 says 'Under Review'. The added expected-completion milestone therefore uses Phase 2's overall window (2026-2031, SocketSite Oct 2020) for the park's larger South/North/West pieces, not a park-specific or Point-specific date.
  • Seawall/shoreline construction: the only statement is Landa's 'building the seawall a few years ago' (Potrero View, Oct 2025); the Phase 1 horizontal work (pile driving, deep soil mixing) was substantially complete May 2023 but the annual report does not itemize shoreline protection.
  • Area: 3.6 acres (D4D, DA, DEIR) vs ~3 acres and '1,000 linear feet' (SF Planning page only) vs 4.0 acres (IMP); the geometry file traces only the visible strip. Not reconciled.
  • The geometry file's 'Waterfront Park' overlaps the newly added Prequel Park (Power Station sub-area); the sources agree the interim park sits within the future waterfront open space, but no drawing on file shows the interim footprint at scale.
  • No construction cost or delivery date for the permanent park; the 3-million-sq-ft trigger is not dated by any source.
  • Port Commission Feb 25, 2020 minutes: the local file is the agenda text; the adoption of Resolution 20-12 is taken from the Nov 2025 memo's recital.

About this shape

Accuracy

APPROXIMATE -- Figure 6.2.3 labels this as a long, curving shoreline strip running the full length of the site's bay frontage (continuing north and south beyond this figure's own page crop in the source PDF). This polygon traces only the portion visible on Figure 6.2.3 itself as a simplified strip (not the full shoreline extent, and not precisely following the actual curving shore/site-boundary line, which was simplified to a handful of vertices). Treat as directionally correct but incomplete and shape-approximate. Same 15-25m positional tolerance as this site's other entries. Position corrected 2026-09-15 (12m uniform shift onto the real shoreline, see digitization_method) -- the strip's own curve/shape still was not independently re-verified vertex-by-vertex against the coastline, only its overall position. Second position correction 2026-09-15: re-traced directly from satellite imagery color (water vs. land pixel classification), not the diagram transform -- now an 8m-wide ribbon on the real shoreline, including the southern point/breakwater curve. Still simplified (does not capture the D4D-labeled strips full north-south extent beyond what is visible here) and the exact width (8m) is an assumption, not measured from any park-design source. NOTE 2026-09-16: this feature's own outer-shoreline trace turned out to substantially overlap the newly-added 'Prequel Park (Power Station sub-area)' -- evidence (a Spring 2026 construction-pipeline document independently describing the same corridor as a '2-acre Waterfront Park') suggests both features describe the same physical shoreline strip: this one as the D4D's own long-term planning label, Prequel Park as the actual interim-construction project now underway. The overlap is expected, not a tracing error -- see Prequel Park (Power Station sub-area)'s own digitization_method. Not merged into one feature since the two have different sourcing/status and either could be independently corrected later.

Why there’s no DataSF parcel

Open space with no subdivided Assessor parcel of its own within the largely unaddressed former power-station site.

Planning context

The site's shoreline public-access open space, per the D4D's own Figure 6.2.3 label, running along the bay side of Blocks 9 and the Stack, connecting toward Warm Water Cove Park to the south (shown on the same figure, outside the project site boundary).
