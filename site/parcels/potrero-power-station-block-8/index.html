Potrero Power Station Block 8

Residential · Potrero Power Station

Also known as 151 Humboldt St

Mid-rise market-rate residential block between Maryland and Delaware streets, south of Humboldt St and north of Power Station Park East, approved March 2, 2022 for a Foster + Partners-designed 14-story, approximately 125-ft, 339-unit building with about 18,000 sq ft of retail; in August 2026 JPMorgan Chase agreed to finance roughly $200 million for a 342-unit building at 151 Humboldt St, with construction expected to begin in fall 2026.1, 10

Open questions

Looked for, not found

  • Address / parcel: the Chronicle gives Block 8 as 151 Humboldt St. status.csv's Block 2 row records that blklot 4232022 (151 Humboldt St) sits roughly where the D4D places Block 8. Block 8 may therefore already have a subdivided Assessor parcel (4232022) that could replace this approximate polygon. Not verified against DataSF here; flagged for the geometry owner.
  • Unit count for the 2026 building (342) differs from the 2022 approval (339) and the compliance reports (348). Whether a revised design review or new site permit has been filed since May 2022 is not documented in the corpus.
  • Architect: Stanley Saitowitz | Natoma Architects publishes a 'Potrero Power Block 8' concept ('initial thoughts to begin the process of developing a design for Block 8', architect's own material, undated). The Chronicle (Aug 2026) still credits Foster + Partners and uses its renderings. Not recorded as a fact; noted here only.
  • Height: the approved plan set shows 125 ft to the last occupied floor with mechanical screening to 145 ft; SF YIMBY reported 140 ft (2021) and 156 ft (2022). The geometry file's note ('140 ft vs. 156 ft') reflects the press figures, not the approval.
  • No construction start found. Searched the 2021-2024 compliance reports, SF YIMBY (Block 8 category, last item March 2022), the Chronicle and SF Standard. The Aug 2026 Chronicle says construction 'is expected to begin in the fall' of 2026; no groundbreaking report yet as of Sept 17, 2026.
  • The geometry file's regulatory_context cites an OEWD pipeline document with 355 units, an estimated start of March 2027 and about $275M; that document is not in the corpus and could not be verified.
  • No general contractor, total development cost, or ground-lease information for Block 8 in any source found (developer-owned land; no Port lease).
  • The 2028 completion figure used for the new expected-completion milestone comes from SF YIMBY's March 2021 report of Planning filings; no source since has restated a completion year for the 342-unit building financed in August 2026, so this is treated as a record-specific but stale estimate rather than a current commitment.

About this shape

Accuracy

APPROXIMATE -- this block is not yet built and does not correspond to any subdivided Assessor parcel: it currently sits on vacant/graded land within the former power station site (unaddressed in DataSF's Assessor roll). Traced from an adopted regulatory diagram via multi-point real-street georeferencing (see digitization_method), but this diagram is dated Feb 2020 and may not reflect later design changes (as already found for the adjacent UCSF/Sophie Maxwell area). Treat vertex positions as accurate to roughly 10-25m, not survey-grade. Rebuilt 2026-09-16 via GDAL GCP/TPS georeferencing -- see digitization_method. Re-measured 2026-09-16 (third pass) in a single consistent pixel-detection pass across the whole block layout -- see digitization_method. Re-corrected 2026-09-16 (fourth pass) after fixing a mis-measured GCP -- see digitization_method.

Traced from

Potrero Power Station Design for Development, Figure 6.2.3 - Building Height Plan (p.245).

Why there’s no DataSF parcel

A real Assessor parcel may exist under this block (part of the site's larger, largely unaddressed footprint), but it has not been subdivided per the masterplan's individual named blocks -- this uses hand-traced approximate geometry instead.

Planning context

Figure 6.2.3 shows Block 8 with a split height: 90 ft (base, small corner) / 125 ft / 85 ft across its width -- the D4D's Permitted Uses table confirms residential is permitted here. Foster + Partners-designed residential building per press coverage, warm metallic patina and rhythmic facade modulation facing Power Station Park per some sources; unit count reported inconsistently across sources (249 vs. 339 vs. a separately-cited 355 units in one SF OEWD pipeline-forecast document, est. start Mar 2027-end May 2029, ~$275M) and height reported inconsistently too (140 ft vs. 156 ft in different press articles) -- confirmed as of a Feb 2026 source that construction had not started.
