Sites/Potrero Station/Louisiana Paseo

Louisiana Paseo

Park · Potrero Power Station

Also known as Louisiana Plaza (Phase 1 application name)

Planned 0.7-acre car-free pedestrian paseo along the east wall of Station A (Block 15), connecting Humboldt Street to 23rd Street and opening onto Power Station Park West; designed with Power Station Park by Plural Studio and approved by the Planning Director on June 23, 2022. A Phase 1 open space delivered with Block 15; not built as of the March 2025 annual report.6, 9, 3, 12

Open questions

Looked for, not found

  • No construction start or completion date found through 2026-09-17; delivery is tied to Block 15 (Station A), whose construction start is not documented in the corpus beyond the 2024 brick stabilization.
  • No cost figure found for the paseo.
  • The DEIR describes the paseo as 'adjacent to Blocks 6 and 10' (2018 block numbering); the D4D/Design Review place it between Block 15 and Blocks 7/11. Block renumbering between DEIR and D4D not traced.
  • The Phase 1 application calls it 'Louisiana Plaza' at 0.63 acres versus 0.7 acres elsewhere; whether this is a scope change or a measurement difference is not stated.
  • Design review submittal date discrepancy ('3.23.20' in annual reports vs March 23, 2021 in the staff memo) as for Power Station Park.

About this shape

Accuracy

APPROXIMATE -- a simplified straight-sided approximation of a landscaped pedestrian paseo; the diagram does not show this as a simple rectangle (it likely has planting/path detail not captured here). Positional accuracy matches whatever the adjoining Block 15/7/11 files currently carry (see their own accuracy_caveat) since this polygon is built directly from their vertices.

Traced from

Potrero Power Station Design for Development, Figure 6.2.3 - Building Height Plan (p.245).

Why there’s no DataSF parcel

Open space with no subdivided Assessor parcel of its own within the largely unaddressed former power-station site.

Planning context

A landscaped pedestrian paseo per the D4D's own Figure 6.2.3 label, connecting Humboldt Street to 23rd Street and opening into Power Station Park.

Research notes

Newly added 2026-09-16; previously mentioned only in passing in other blocks' notes, never tracked as its own feature.
