Prequel Park (Power Station sub-area)

Park · Potrero Power Station

Also known as Prequel Park, The Stack at Prequel Park

Developer-owned portion of Prequel Park, the ~2-acre (2.75 acres per press) interim park designed with Heatherwick Studio around Unit 3 and the 300-foot Stack on the waterfront area entitled for a hotel; the Port memo of Nov 2025 targets construction in 2026 and opening in 2027, and the project's BCDC amendment was issued June 26, 2026. No source found confirms construction has started.9, 3, 11, 2

Open questions

Looked for, not found

  • Status: the geometry file says 'under_construction'. No dated source found confirms a construction start as of 2026-09-17; the latest primary statement is the Port memo (Nov 2025, start Q1 or Q3 2026) and ICA SF ('Construction begins in 2026', Oct 2025). Recommend 'planned' until a dated source appears.
  • Area: ~2 acres (Port memo), 2.5 acres (developer site), 2.75 acres (Chronicle, ICA SF), 'almost three' (Potrero View); the BCDC amendment scope is 57,000-62,000 sq ft within the shoreline band only. The developer-land share is not stated anywhere (the ~1.6 acres in the geometry file is a subtraction, not a sourced figure).
  • The 'Spring 2026 SF development pipeline' listing cited in the geometry file (developer FIFTH SPACE; scope demolition, grading, utilities, stormwater, landscaping) could not be found online; no URL on file.
  • BCDC 2019.006.02: received June 19, 2025, issued June 26, 2026 per the BCDC tracker; the repo currently records June 19, 2025 as the issue date. The amendment text itself was not found.
  • Whether the 2024 annual report's 'Delaware East Park/Stack Plaza' temporary activation (Andrea Cochran Landscape Architecture, schematic design from Aug 2024) is the same scheme later branded Prequel Park with Heatherwick Studio is not stated by any source.
  • Application filing: Chronicle (May 22, 2025) says the application was expected to be filed 'on Thursday'; SF YIMBY (Oct 2025) says 'In May, the developer submitted plans'. No Planning case number found.
  • Cost: only the developer's own $15-20 million estimate (Chronicle, Potrero View). No construction contract or permit valuation found.
  • BOS File 260724 (Amended and Restated DA, noticed Sept 11, 2026) amends the Phasing Plan; whether it changes hotel or interim-park obligations is not stated in the legislative digest, and the draft DA text was not read.

About this shape

Accuracy

APPROXIMATE -- this polygon combines two different sourcing methods (see digitization_method) and has not been independently re-verified as a whole against satellite imagery. Its west edge is exact (shared vertices with Block 4/Humboldt Street Plaza/Block 9). Its east/shoreline edge inherits whatever accuracy Waterfront Park's own trace has -- treat it as a reasonable but not freshly-verified approximation of the real shoreline. This is the 'Power Station sub-area' complement to 'Prequel Park (Port sub-area)' -- together they approximate the full ~2-acre interim park project, though the true internal boundary between Port and Power-Station land has not been independently confirmed.

Traced from

Potrero Power Station Design for Development, Figure 6.2.3 (shoreline shown as 'Waterfront Park'); SF Port Commission Nov 2025 memo and Spring 2026 development-forecast listing for 'Potrero Power Station - Prequel Park' (2-acre interim waterfront park).

Why there’s no DataSF parcel

Open space with no subdivided Assessor parcel of its own within the largely unaddressed former power-station site.

Planning context

The Potrero Power Station-land portion of the ~2-acre Prequel Park interim open-space project (see 'Prequel Park (Port sub-area)' for the Port-jurisdiction portion and its own sourcing). Construction per the Spring 2026 SF development-pipeline forecast (developer: FIFTH SPACE); scope includes demolition/site clearing, earthwork and grading, underground utilities, stormwater treatment, and landscaping.

Research notes

Newly added 2026-09-16 to complement 'Prequel Park (Port sub-area)' -- see accuracy_caveat.
