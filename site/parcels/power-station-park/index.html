Power Station Park

Park · Potrero Power Station

Also known as Power Station Park East, Power Station Park West

Planned ~1.2-acre central park in two blocks either side of Maryland Street: East between Blocks 8 and 12 (multi-purpose lawn and plaza opening to Unit 3 and the Stack) and West between Blocks 7 and 11 (play plaza). Schematic design by Plural Studio was approved by the Planning Director on June 23, 2022 (Case 2017-011878PHA-03). A Phase 1 open space delivered with Blocks 8 (East) and 11 (West); as of the March 2025 annual report neither block nor the park was under construction.7, 10, 3, 2

Open questions

Looked for, not found

  • No construction start, groundbreaking or completion date for either park block was found in any source through 2026-09-17; the park is tied to Blocks 8 and 11, and Block 8 was still seeking financing in Aug 2026. The geometry file's 'not yet built' is consistent with the March 2025 annual report but nothing later confirms it. The added expected-completion milestone therefore uses Phase 1's overall window (2028, SocketSite Oct 2020), not a park-specific date.
  • No cost figure for Power Station Park was found (annual reports give contract values only for streets, Unit 3, Sophie Maxwell, Station A).
  • Landscape architect identification rests on the 'plural' logo and 2724 17th Street address on the 7.29.21 plan sheets plus CMG's 'Affiliated Landscape Architect: Plural Studio'; no document names the firm in text.
  • Annual reports 2021-2023 print the design review submittal date as '3.23.20'; the Planning staff memo says March 23, 2021. The memo date is used.
  • The 2021 annual report lists Power Station Park East as 'Design review approved, 8.3.21' delivered with Block 12; from 2022 it is listed as delivered with Block 8 with a site permit application. The reason for the change is not stated.
  • Phase 1 application table (Table 5) lists Power Station Park West as moved from DA Phase 2 to Phase 1; the East block does not appear in that change table (presumably already Phase 1) - not confirmed against DA Exhibit M.
  • BOS File 260724 (Amended and Restated DA, 2026) amends the Phasing Plan; effects on park delivery triggers not checked (draft DA not read).

About this shape

Accuracy

APPROXIMATE -- Figure 6.2.3 labels this open space directly but shows it as an irregular, non-rectangular shape woven between Blocks 7, 8, 9, 11, and 12; this is a simplified two-part rectangular approximation (split 2026-09-16 by the real Maryland Street corridor that separates the Block 7/11 side from the Block 8/12 side -- see digitization_method), not a precise trace of the diagram's own organic outline. Each part shares an exact edge with its own two blocks by construction, so accuracy tracks whatever those block files carry.

Traced from

Potrero Power Station Design for Development, Figure 6.2.3 - Building Height Plan (p.245).

Why there’s no DataSF parcel

Open space with no subdivided Assessor parcel of its own within the largely unaddressed former power-station site.

Planning context

The site's central public open space, per the D4D's own Figure 6.2.3 label -- sits between the Humboldt St row of blocks (7/8/9) and the 23rd St row (11/12). Not yet built (the blocks around it are still 'planned').

Research notes

Simplified rectangular approximation of a labeled but irregularly-shaped open space -- see accuracy_caveat.
