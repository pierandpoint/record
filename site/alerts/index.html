Alerts

Breaking-news alerts

The monthly digest is the default: one email, everything that changed. Breaking-news alerts are optional, for the rare thing that shouldn’t wait a month. This page is the whole bar for sending one -- not a summary of it, the actual rule.

What qualifies

  • A public hearing or comment deadline on a covered site, coming up within about two weeks. A record’s own “How to comment” section, where one exists, is what this draws from.
  • A major project’s status changing: approved, breaking ground, opening, paused or cancelled.
  • A new proposal or project appearing on, or next to, one of the four sites.

What never qualifies

A minor permit. A correction to our own record, or an addition to our own coverage -- those are Pier & Point keeping its own house in order, not news about a site, and they show up in the digest’s own Corrections and New-on-the-record sections instead.

How it works

An alert is one fact, one source, about one record, in the same look as the digest. It is built and drafted the same way the digest is: a person runs it by hand (there is no automatic trigger for an alert, ever), it lands as an unsent draft in the Buttondown dashboard, and a person reviews and sends it. At most two alerts go out in any calendar month; if nothing clears the bar above, none do.

Alerts are opt-in and separate from the topics you choose for the digest -- turning alerts on doesn’t change what the monthly digest covers, and turning it off doesn’t unsubscribe you from the digest. How preferences are stored.
