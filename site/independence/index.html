Independence

Independence policy

Pier & Point is useful only if people can trust it, including when the record is inconvenient for the people building on the waterfront. This page says what that means in practice.

Who runs this

Pier & Point is run by Pierre Rohel, who lives in the neighborhood. By day he is a Principal Software Security Engineer at ServiceNow, working on security architecture for its AI platform; that work has nothing to do with real estate or the waterfront.

Several billion dollars of city are going up within walking distance of my front door, and I wanted to watch it happen. More than once I stood at a fence and couldn’t work out what was coming, where, or when. The information exists, but it is spread across Port packets, Planning approvals, Assessor records and press releases, on different dates and in different places. If that was frustrating for me, it was frustrating for other people too. So I built the thing I wanted: one map, every claim with a source.

I have no financial interest in any of the sites covered here. If I ever do paid work for a party that appears in this record, it is done under a separate, named brand and disclosed on this page; it has no bearing on what this site says.

What we don’t do

  • Pier & Point is free, public and the same for everyone. It takes no money from any developer, landowner or public agency whose sites it covers, and sells nothing to them: no sponsorship, no advertising, no paid listings, no consulting.
  • No one who is covered sees a change before it is public, or gets to approve how their project is described.
  • Records are never softened or removed at someone’s request. Errors are corrected, with sources, on the public changelog.
  • We accept no gifts, hospitality or access in return for coverage. Public documents, press releases and site visits open to anyone are fair game; private briefings on the condition of favourable treatment are not.

What we do

  • Every status, date and shape cites a primary source, and the method page says how the record is kept.
  • When we are wrong, the correction is dated and sourced on the changelog, next to everyone else’s. Corrections stay visible; history is never rewritten.
  • We treat everyone we cover the same: the Port, the City, the developers, the tenants and the neighbours.
  • We also record proposals next to the sites we track, their process and who has said what about them. We don’t take positions on them.

Related work

Any other work the founder does, including paid work for a party that appears in this record, is done under a separate, named brand, not as Pier & Point, and is disclosed on this page: the brand, the common ownership and the client. It has no bearing on the record. No client sees anything before it is public, no client has a say in how anything here is described, and every client gets the public data on the same terms as everyone else. At the time of writing there is no such brand or client to list; when there is, it will be named here.

Questions

If you think something on this site is influenced by anyone it covers, tell us. We will answer, and if a change is warranted it goes on the changelog.
