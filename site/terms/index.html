Terms and disclaimer

Using Pier & Point means agreeing to the terms below. They are written to be read; if any part is unclear, ask.

What this site is

Pier & Point is an independent, informational public record of development on San Francisco’s central waterfront: Mission Rock, Mission Bay, Pier 70 and the Potrero Power Station. It is compiled from public sources, which are cited on every record: Port of San Francisco and City and County of San Francisco documents, SF Planning records, DataSF datasets, adopted design documents, and reporting that cites them. It is provided as is, for general information, free of charge.

Not advice, no warranty

Nothing on this site is legal, financial, real estate, construction, engineering, safety, planning or investment advice, and nothing on it is an offer or solicitation of any kind. Do not rely on it for any decision with consequences; check the original sources and the responsible public agencies first.

We work hard to cite and check everything, and we say plainly what is uncertain, but statuses, dates, shapes, names, figures and descriptions can be incomplete, out of date or wrong. Everything on the site is presented to the best of our knowledge, and true to the sources we cite. In particular:

  • Approximate shapes (drawn hatched on the maps) are traced from scaled planning figures, not surveyed. Each carries its own note on how it was traced and how accurate it is likely to be. They are not property boundaries.
  • Site outlines on the overview map are drawn around the records we track. They are not official project or jurisdiction boundaries.
  • Dates before today come only from dated, cited milestones; where no source gives a date, the record shows none. Dates after today are targets stated by the sources cited, not predictions by us.
  • Counts (records complete, under construction, planned) are counts of records, not of homes, floor area or land, and depend on how the record is divided.

To the fullest extent permitted by law, the site is provided without warranties of any kind, express or implied, including accuracy, completeness, fitness for a particular purpose and non-infringement, and Pier & Point is not liable for any loss or damage arising from use of, or reliance on, the site or its data.

Corrections

Errors are corrected, not quietly overwritten. Every change to the record is dated and sourced on the public changelog, including our own mistakes; the full history is kept. Records are never softened or removed at the request of anyone they concern. We correct a record when a primary source shows it is wrong, and we say so.

Reporting an error

Tell us at feedback@pierandpoint.org or through the feedback page, ideally with a source we can check. We aim to acknowledge reports within a week and to act on confirmed ones promptly. Confirmed corrections are credited on the changelog if you would like.

Not affiliated

Pier & Point is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting for the Port of San Francisco, the City and County of San Francisco, the Office of Community Investment and Infrastructure (OCII), or any developer, landowner, tenant or agency mentioned on the site. Project, company and place names are used only to identify what the record describes. Our independence policy says what we do and don’t accept from the people we cover.

Sources, images and data

Source documents, images and third-party data belong to their respective owners and are credited where they appear. Images hosted on this site are public-record documents or openly licensed files, each with a credit and a license; anything else is linked to, not copied. Map data includes City and County of San Francisco records published through DataSF and street data © OpenStreetMap contributors (Open Database License).

Our own compilation, text and drawn shapes may be quoted, shared and reused with attribution to Pier & Point and a link to the record page, without implying that we endorse the use. Each record page has a “Cite this record” line for that purpose. Wholesale republication of the site, or use that presents our data as official, is not permitted.

Privacy

No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for to read the site, and no cookies are set by it.

Analytics. The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless. It records, for each page view: the page address, the referring page, the browser and device type, the country the visit came from, and page-load timings. It sets no cookies, does not fingerprint devices, does not track you across other sites, and gives us aggregates only; we never see who you are. Cloudflare, which also hosts the site, receives your IP address to serve it, as any web host does, and retains its own server logs under its privacy policy.

Fonts. The site’s typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details for that request and no more. We may switch to hosting the fonts ourselves.

The monthly digest. If you subscribe, your email address is held by Buttondown, the newsletter service we use, and used only to send the digest. Open and click tracking is switched off. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing deletes you from the list. We do not sell, rent or share the list.

Feedback. Email you send us, and web-form messages (which reach us through Formspree), are kept only as long as it takes to act on them, and are never published without asking you. If you ask to be credited for a correction, the name you give appears on the changelog.

Your rights. To see, correct or delete anything we hold about you, email feedback@pierandpoint.org.

Closed beta

During the closed beta, access is by invitation. Please don’t share screenshots or content publicly without asking, and expect the record to change as it is checked.
