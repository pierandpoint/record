Glossary

Acronyms and terms of art used on this site, expanded in plain words the first time each one appears on a page.

ADAAmericans with Disabilities Act

A federal civil-rights law that protects people with disabilities in many areas of public life, with published accessibility Design Standards.

ada.gov

AFDMAuxiliary Floating Dry Dock, Medium

A US Navy type designation for a medium-sized floating dry dock, like the vessel now known as the Eureka Dry Dock.

AIAAmerican Institute of Architects

A national professional association for architects; some record pages cite its design awards.

aia.org

AMIArea Median Income

The median income for the region: half of households earn more, half less. Below-market-rate homes limit household income by reference to it.

huduser.gov/portal/datasets/il.html

BCDCBay Conservation and Development Commission

The state agency that issues permits for work within its jurisdiction around San Francisco Bay.

bcdc.ca.gov

BDABi-Directional Amplifier

A device that boosts radio signal strength inside a building so fire and police radios still work throughout it, often installed alongside an ERRCS.

BLFBoat Launch Facility

A public ramp or dock for launching small boats, as California State Parks' boating division names it.

BMRBelow Market Rate

A home priced and restricted for lower- or moderate-income households under San Francisco's affordable-housing rules.

sf.gov/reports--august-2024--below-market-rate-bmr-ownership-programs

BOSBoard of Supervisors

San Francisco's elected city council, which sets city policy and adopts ordinances and resolutions.

sfbos.org

CACCommunity Advisory Committee

A standing group of neighbors and stakeholders an agency consults as a project moves forward.

CC0CC0 1.0 Universal

A public-domain dedication: the copyright holder gives up all rights, so the work can be reused freely with no credit required.

creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0

CEQACalifornia Environmental Quality Act

The state law requiring public agencies to study and disclose a project's environmental effects before approving it.

lci.ca.gov/ceqa

CFDCommunity Facilities District

An area where property owners pay a special tax, secured by bonds, to fund infrastructure like streets, parks and utilities.

treasurer.ca.gov/cdiac/mello-roos/index.asp

CIPCapital Improvement Program

An agency's multi-year plan for funding and building physical projects, like the Port's Five-Year CIP.

CMBSCommercial Mortgage-Backed Securities

A loan on a commercial building that's bundled with other such loans and sold to investors as bonds.

CPUCCalifornia Public Utilities Commission

The state agency that regulates privately owned utilities such as PG&E.

cpuc.ca.gov

D4DDesign for Development

The illustrated rulebook a developer and the city agree on for a site: building heights, uses, streets and parks.

sfplanning.org/potrero-power-station

DBIDepartment of Building Inspection

San Francisco's department that reviews and inspects construction to enforce the building code.

sfdbi.org

DDADisposition and Development Agreement

The principal agreement between a public landowner, such as the Port, and a developer, setting out the overall program and timing of development and the public benefits to be delivered.

sfport.com/sites/default/files/Commission/Item%2011A%20Mission%20Rock%20Approvals%20Staff%20Report%201-26-18FINAL.pdf

DEIRDraft Environmental Impact Report

The draft version of a project's environmental impact report, issued before the final version.

sfplanning.org/environmental-review

DRBDesign Review Board

The Bay Conservation and Development Commission's advisory board of design experts, which reviews how a shoreline project treats public access and the water's edge.

bcdc.ca.gov/advisory-groups/design-review-board

DSADivision of the State Architect

The California state agency that reviews and approves construction plans for public schools and certain other state-related buildings.

dgs.ca.gov/DSA

DTSCDepartment of Toxic Substances Control

The California agency that oversees cleanup of contaminated land, common on former industrial waterfront sites.

dtsc.ca.gov

EIFDEnhanced Infrastructure Financing District

A district in which growth in property-tax revenue is set aside to pay for infrastructure; the Board of Supervisors created one at the Potrero Power Station.

sfstandard.com/2025/10/15/potrero-power-station-middle-income-housing

EIREnvironmental Impact Report

The formal study, under the California Environmental Quality Act, of a project's potential adverse environmental effects.

sfplanning.org/environmental-review

ENAExclusive Negotiating Agreement

An agreement giving a developer the exclusive right to negotiate a deal on a public site for a set period, before any lease or sale is final.

EPAEnvironmental Protection Agency

The federal agency that regulates pollution and oversees cleanup of contaminated sites.

epa.gov

ERRCSEmergency Responder Radio Coverage System

In-building equipment required by the fire code so police and fire radios work throughout a building, including basements and stairwells.

ESEREarthquake Safety and Emergency Response

A series of San Francisco general-obligation bonds funding seismic upgrades to fire stations, police facilities and other emergency infrastructure.

sfpublicworks.org/eser

FTAFederal Transit Administration

The U.S. Department of Transportation agency that funds public transit projects nationwide.

transit.dot.gov

GCPGround Control Point

A point on the ground whose real-world coordinates are known, used to align a scanned map or aerial photo to its true position.

GDALGeospatial Data Abstraction Library

A free, open-source (MIT-style licence) translator library for raster and vector geospatial data formats.

gdal.org

GISGeographic Information System

Software that stores and maps real-world locations and shapes -- the kind of tool behind this site's own maps.

usgs.gov/faqs/what-geographic-information-system-gis

GSFGross Square Feet

The total floor area of a building, measured to the outside of its exterior walls.

HAERHistoric American Engineering Record

A National Park Service program, founded in 1969, that documents America's historic engineering and industrial sites.

nps.gov/subjects/heritagedocumentation/haer.htm

HPCHistoric Preservation Commission

The San Francisco commission, established by the City Charter in 2008, that advises the Mayor, Board of Supervisors and City departments on historic preservation goals, policies and programs.

sfplanning.org/historic-preservation-commission

HVACHeating, Ventilation and Air Conditioning

The building systems that control indoor temperature, humidity and air quality.

IFDInfrastructure Financing District

An area where growth in property-tax revenue is set aside to pay for new infrastructure, without raising tax rates.

IRFDInfrastructure and Revitalization Financing District

A financing district authorized by state law in 2014 (Assembly Bill 229) to finance housing and other development projects.

scag.ca.gov/post/infrastructure-and-revitalization-financing-district-irfd

LBELocal Business Enterprise

A local business certified under San Francisco's Chapter 14B program, which helps small businesses compete for City contracts.

sf.gov/14b-local-business-enterprise-lbe-program

LDDALease Disposition and Development Agreement

A Port of San Francisco development agreement paired with a master lease of Port land, as used for the Pier 70 Historic Core rehabilitation.

sfport.com/media/4465/download

LEDLight Emitting Diode

A small, energy-efficient light source; cited here from a building permit's own description of fire-alarm signal hardware.

LEEDLeadership in Energy and Environmental Design

A widely used green-building certification, rated Certified, Silver, Gold or Platinum.

usgbc.org/leed

LRDPLong Range Development Plan

UCSF's own campus-wide master plan -- where it can build and how much -- reviewed under state environmental law.

realestate.ucsf.edu/long-range-development-plan-lrdp

LRPMPLong Range Property Management Plan

The plan a former redevelopment agency's successor, like OCII, submits for the disposition of the property it still holds.

sfocii.org/sites/default/files/inline-files/02%20Mission%20Bay.pdf

MEPMechanical, Electrical and Plumbing

The building trades and engineering disciplines covering a building's heating, cooling, power and water systems.

MGDMillion Gallons per Day

A standard way to measure how much water a treatment plant or pump station can handle.

MOHCDMayor's Office of Housing and Community Development

The San Francisco agency that supports affordable housing opportunities and manages the city's below-market-rate ownership programs.

sf.gov/reports--august-2024--below-market-rate-bmr-ownership-programs

MOUMemorandum of Understanding

A written agreement laying out what two or more parties plan to do together, short of a binding contract.

NAIPNational Agriculture Imagery Program

A USDA program that photographs the continental United States from the air during growing seasons, on a multi-year cycle -- a common public-domain source for aerial images on this site.

en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Agriculture_Imagery_Program

NFPANational Fire Protection Association

A nonprofit focused on fire, electrical and related hazards; permit descriptions on this site cite its standards, such as NFPA 24 for underground fire-sprinkler water service.

NHPNational Historical Park

A US National Park Service unit preserving a historic site, like the San Francisco Maritime NHP.

NOENotice of Exemption

A filing an agency makes when it determines a project is exempt from full environmental review under CEQA.

NRHPNational Register of Historic Places

The federal government's official list of buildings, sites and districts worth preserving for their history.

nps.gov/subjects/nationalregister/database-research.htm

NRISNational Register Information System

The National Park Service's internal workflow system for tracking National Register of Historic Places documentation; its reports carry a reference number for each property.

nps.gov/subjects/nationalregister/upload/NR-NHL-NRIS-terms_20210805.pdf

OCIIOffice of Community Investment and Infrastructure

San Francisco's successor to the Redevelopment Agency, which still oversees Mission Bay and other large approved project areas.

sfocii.org/what-we-do

OEWDOffice of Economic and Workforce Development

San Francisco's department for business support, job training and local economic development.

oewd.org

OPAOwner Participation Agreement

A redevelopment-era agreement between the former redevelopment agency and a master developer that authorized a development and set what the developer must build, as at Mission Bay in 1998.

sfocii.org/sites/default/files/inline-files/02%20Mission%20Bay.pdf

OSMOpenStreetMap

A free, editable map of the world built by volunteers; the background tiles of this site's interactive map come from it.

openstreetmap.org/about

OTCOver the Counter

A Department of Building Inspection permit reviewed over the counter, for smaller-scope work that doesn't need a full plan review.

PDRProduction, Distribution and Repair

San Francisco's planning term for industrial uses that make, move or fix things, the subject of a Planning Department report on the city's remaining industrial land.

sfplanning.org/resource/industrial-land-san-francisco-production-distribution-and-repair-pdr

PWPPacific Waterfront Partners

The Port of San Francisco tenant and developer affiliate behind the Piers 38 & 40 redevelopment proposal.

RFIRequest for Information

An informal request an agency sends out to gather information before deciding how to proceed.

RFPRequest for Proposals

A public agency's formal invitation for companies to submit competing proposals for a project or contract.

RFQRequest for Qualifications

A public agency's formal call for firms to submit their credentials, often the step before a full proposal.

RMSRoot Mean Square

A standard way to summarize error: the square root of the mean of the squared differences, here between a traced shape and its true position.

RPDRecreation and Park Department

San Francisco's department that manages the city's parks.

sfrecpark.org

RSFRentable Square Feet

The floor area a tenant is billed for, including a share of shared spaces like lobbies and hallways.

SBASmall Business Administration

The federal agency that certifies and supports small businesses, including some contractors named on this site.

sba.gov

SBESmall Business Enterprise

A small-business contracting category; on this site it appears in OCII's project contracting goals, alongside local hire.

SCHState Clearinghouse

The California office that receives environmental review filings; each project is referenced by a State Clearinghouse Number (SCH#).

lci.ca.gov/sch

SECSecurities and Exchange Commission

The federal agency that oversees the securities markets and protects investors; some facts here are sourced to a company's own SEC filings.

sec.gov/about/mission

SFCTASan Francisco County Transportation Authority

The city agency that plans and funds San Francisco's transportation projects, including some sales-tax (Prop L) funding decisions.

sfcta.org

SFFDSan Francisco Fire Department

San Francisco's fire department, which also responds to medical emergencies.

sf-fire.org

SFMTASan Francisco Municipal Transportation Agency

San Francisco's transit and streets agency, running Muni and setting traffic and parking rules.

sfmta.com

SFPDSan Francisco Police Department

San Francisco's police department.

sanfranciscopolice.org

SFPLSan Francisco Public Library

San Francisco's public library system.

sfpl.org

SFPWSan Francisco Public Works

The city department that builds and maintains streets and public infrastructure.

sfpublicworks.org

SFUSDSan Francisco Unified School District

San Francisco's public school system.

sfusd.edu

SFWPSan Francisco Waterfront Partners

The Port of San Francisco's development partner for the Piers 38 & 40 redevelopment.

SLRSea Level Rise

The long-term rise in the bay's water level that waterfront projects have to design and build for.

SUDSpecial Use District

An extra layer of zoning rules laid over a specific area, on top of or instead of the normal citywide rules.

codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/san_francisco/latest/sf_planning/0-0-0-19790

TCOTemporary Certificate of Occupancy

A short-term permit letting a building be used before every last item of construction is finished.

TPSThin Plate Spline

A flexible way to stretch a scanned map onto real-world coordinates, allowing for small warps rather than just a shift and rotation.

TSFTransportation Sustainability Fee

A San Francisco development impact fee, charged on new construction, that funds transit, walking and biking improvements.

UCSFUniversity of California, San Francisco

The public university's health-sciences campus, a major landowner and developer at Mission Bay.

ucsf.edu

USDAUnited States Department of Agriculture

The federal department that runs the aerial-imagery program behind some of this site's satellite-style photos.

usda.gov

VDDAVertical Disposition and Development Agreement

The agreement, attached as a form to a site's DDA, that sets the conditions for a vertical developer to acquire a development parcel's lease and build on it.

sfport.com/sites/default/files/Commission/Item%2011A%20Mission%20Rock%20Approvals%20Staff%20Report%201-26-18FINAL.pdf

WETASan Francisco Bay Area Water Emergency Transportation Authority

The regional public agency that runs Bay Area ferry service and builds ferry terminals.

weta.sanfranciscobayferry.com

YIMBYYes In My Backyard

A pro-housing-development political movement, and on this site also the name of a local construction-news site, SF YIMBY.

en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YIMBY
