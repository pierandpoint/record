Feedback and corrections

Spotted an error, a missing record, or a status that has moved on? Tell us, ideally with a source. Confirmed corrections go on the changelog with their source, and are credited if you’d like.

By email

Email feedback@pierandpoint.org

If that button doesn’t open your mail app, the address is feedback@pierandpoint.org.

By form

The web form is not switched on yet. Email works now, and every message is read.

What happens next

  • We check the claim against a primary source before changing anything.
  • Corrections are dated and sourced on the changelog. Records are corrected, never quietly overwritten (see Method).
  • Nobody who is covered by the record gets to approve how their project is described (see Independence).

Monthly digest

One email a month: what changed on the record, what’s next. No tracking pixels; unsubscribe in one click. How your address is handled.
