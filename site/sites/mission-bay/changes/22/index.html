  • Apr 6, 2018StatusMission Bay

    Mission Creek Park South Extension, Parcel P2: Hoodline, published April 6, 2018, says a Mission Creek houseboat resident "makes up for it by getting involved at nearby Huffaker Park" (after saying she misses "a real garden with soil"), so a Huffaker Park was in use then. The article does not say where the park is. Rec and Park's schematic design (undated) calls Huffaker Park "existing" and says it "will be preserved and incorporated into the park design", and gives "The existing community garden is 11,500 square feet." It also says "the existing Huffaker Park path will remain in the same location and width while the paving material will be required to be improved to city standards". The date is the article's, which shows the park in use; it is not the date the park was built. The park stands today but is not at its final size: the planned extension builds around it and may rework part of it. No City page found says in its own words that the park is open today.

    Hoodline, Apr 6, 2018; San Francisco Recreation and Parks Department (document names Catellus and the San Francisco Redevelopment Agency)
