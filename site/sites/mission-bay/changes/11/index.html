  • Oct 10, 2026CorrectionMission Bay

    UCSF's Wayne and Gladys Valley Center for Vision at 490 Illinois St is the block 33 building between Third St and Illinois St, not the pair of buildings east of Illinois St that this project had listed under that name. The City's address point for 490 Illinois St falls inside the block 33 outline; UCSF's 2025 campus map labels block 33 'Valley Center for Vision'; SF YIMBY places the facility 'along 3rd Street, immediately across from the UCSF Medical Center'; and the two buildings east of Illinois St were already standing, about six stories tall, in the City's 2010 building survey. The block 33 record is renamed 'UCSF Wayne and Gladys Valley Center for Vision' and now carries the 2017 Regents approval, the November 2020 opening, the $237 million cost, the SmithGroup design, the 347,500 sq ft size and the Caffe Central tenant. Its old page address redirects to the new one. View record →
