  • Oct 11, 2026CorrectionMission Bay

    The page said the UC Regents approved the Block 33 building's budget and design on January 26, 2017, citing a UCSF news page that was not read (and whose listed access date was wrong). It now cites the Regents' own records, which were read: the minutes of January 26, 2017 show the Board approving the Finance and Capital Strategies Committee's recommendations, which include a $237.13 million budget and the building's design. The agenda item gives a proposed schedule of completion by July 2019, with construction starting in June 2017. The Regents' documents call it the Mission Bay East Campus Phase 1 Building (Block 33), not the Valley Center for Vision, and describe a 343,000-gross-square-foot building, mostly academic and administrative workspace; the page now lists that as an open question. View record →

  • Oct 11, 2026CorrectionMission Bay

    The page said 499 Illinois Street is Assessor lot 3940005. DataSF's records show lot 3940004 addressed as 499 Illinois St, lot 3940005 addressed as 409 to 499 Illinois St, and lot 3940003 addressed as 409 Illinois St, all active with one shared outline. Lot 3940001, which the 2006 building permit used, is retired. The page no longer speculates about lot-line adjustments. It also gave the dentistry school's date as 'targeted to open in late 2029'. American School & University magazine reports a completion date targeted for late 2029; SF Standard reports the building ready for occupants in the first quarter of 2029. The page now gives both, and the September 28, 2026 entry on this page that credited the late-2029 wording to SF Standard was wrong. View record →
