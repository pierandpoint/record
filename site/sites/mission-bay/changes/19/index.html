    Parklab Gardens & Mission Bay Commons (interim, west): Parklab Gardens (one interim installation inside the mapped Parklab park strip) is reported as "opening Friday" in an SF Chronicle article of Aug 17, 2018 that pairs it with Stagecoach Greens mini-golf; the article is a pre-opening report, so the month is approximate and no later source gives the opening day.
