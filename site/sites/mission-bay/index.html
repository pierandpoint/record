Mission Bay

OCII redevelopment area

Mission Bay's 238 acres were formerly underutilized Southern Pacific Railroad rail yards and surrounding industrial properties.1 OCII's Mission Bay Redevelopment Plan is converting the area into a new, 238-acre, mixed-income, transit-oriented neighborhood of residential, commercial and institutional uses.1 Mission Bay School (the neighborhood's first public school) opened Aug 17 2026; Block 12 West -- the project area's last undeveloped parcel -- is now going through an OCII subdivision MOU.2
