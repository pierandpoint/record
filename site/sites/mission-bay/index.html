Mission Bay

OCII redevelopment area

87 of 103 tracked complete · 0 under constructionRecord as of Oct 12, 2026

Pier & Point is an independent, cited record of waterfront development. How we work →

Mission Bay was formerly underutilized Southern Pacific railyards and surrounding properties.1 The Mission Bay North and South Redevelopment Plans, approved in 1998 and administered by OCII, are converting the area into a new neighborhood; Mission Bay South alone is a 238-acre, mixed-income, transit-oriented residential, commercial and institutional district.1 Mission Bay School, the neighborhood's first public school campus, held its grand opening on Aug 6, 2026 and was scheduled to welcome its first students on Aug 17; Block 12 West, the last remaining development parcel in Mission Bay South, is the subject of a proposed OCII memorandum of understanding that had not yet been approved as of an Aug 13, 2026 advisory committee item.2, 3
