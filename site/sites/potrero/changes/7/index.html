  • ~1965RetailPotrero Station

    The Stack (Unit 3 smokestack): Unit 3 Power Block and the Boiler Stack dated 1965 in the 2018 Draft EIR ('both constructed in 1965'), which extended the Third Street Industrial District's period of significance to 1965 and added both as contributing resources; the DEIR's project description gives 'ca. 1965', and press reports give 1962 for the power plant (see open questions)

    SF Planning Department, 2018-10; San Francisco Chronicle (Laura Waxmann), 2025-05-22; SF YIMBY, 2025-10-24
  • ~1965StatusPotrero Station

    The Stack (Unit 3 smokestack): The Boiler Stack and the adjoining Unit 3 Power Block (dated 1965 in the 2018 Draft EIR; press reports give 1962 for the power plant) are covered by the final Design for Development (Feb 26, 2020) as existing structures: Standard 6.2.1, 'Height of Existing Structures', sets the Stack's height limit at its existing height and provides that a collapsed or irreparably damaged Stack is not replaced by a new structure of that height

    SF Planning Department, 2018-10; SF Planning, 2020-02-26
  • ~1901MilestonePotrero Station

    Potrero Power Station Block 15 / Station A: Station A built in 1901-02, with a 1930-31 addition (Draft EIR Table 4.D-1 gives its construction date as '1901-02; 1930-31' and does not say when work began; a 1900 Sanborn map notation records the site being excavated for the plant); individually eligible for the California Register under Criterion 1, and a contributor to the Third Street Industrial District

    SF Planning Department, 2018-10
  • ~1901StatusPotrero Station

    Potrero Power Station Block 15 / Station A: Station A's original construction begins (completed 1902, with a 1930-31 addition), predating the Potrero Power Station redevelopment plan by more than a century; the building is retained today (rather than demolished) for adaptive reuse as an office building

    SF Planning Department, 2018-10; SF Planning Department, 2022-03-02
