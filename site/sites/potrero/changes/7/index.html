  • May 2023MilestonePotrero Station

    Waterfront Park: Demolition, rough grading and shoreline improvements by DeSilva Gates substantially complete: the 2023 annual report (letter of Nov 22, 2024) says DGC 'substantially completed the Horizontal Construction Project, which includes demolition, rough grading, and shoreline improvements, between July 2021 and May 2023' and that 'In May 2023, CBC wrapped up the first phase of the Project's shoreline improvements, which included deep soil mixing (DSM), approximately 800 linear feet of seawall construction, installation of steel pipe piles for the future dock, riprap slope protection, and the storm drain outfall'; the 2024 annual report (Mar 2025) says the demolition, excavation, abatement, pile driving and deep soil mixing work 'was substantially complete in May 2023', yet still lists the Horizontal Construction Project among contracts underway at December 2024 and says no Phase 1 horizontal infrastructure is complete. The seawall and shoreline work runs along the Bay frontage where the waterfront open spaces are planned; the reports do not say which parts lie within Waterfront Park, The Point or the Prequel Park band, and the Phase 1 application describes early work as 'geotechnical improvements along the shoreline and in areas of fill'. None of this is landscape construction of the park itself

    California Barrel Company LLC (developer's compliance report, posted by SF Planning, M-Files), Nov 22, 2024; California Barrel Company LLC (developer's compliance report, posted by SF Planning, M-Files), 2025-03; California Barrel Company / SF Planning (M-Files: 'Potrero Power Station Phase 1 and Phasing Plan Amendment Applications'), 2020-10
