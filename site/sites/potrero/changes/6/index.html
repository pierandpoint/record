  • Apr 21, 2020MilestonePotrero Station

    Prequel Park (Power Station sub-area): Board of Supervisors approves the Potrero Power Station project and Development Agreement (Ordinance No. 62-20, Board File 200040), the overall entitlement for the site on which Prequel Park was later proposed (in 2025); neither cited source ties the approval to this sub-area; the Development Agreement's own effective date is May 25, 2020, distinct from the D4D's own February 26, 2020 final-document date

    Port of San Francisco, 2025-11-14; SF Planning
  • Feb 26, 2020RetailPotrero Station

    The Stack (Unit 3 smokestack): Final Design for Development sets the Stack's height limit at its existing height, and provides that if the Stack collapses or is damaged beyond repair, the 300-ft height limit will not apply to a replacement and the land will instead be used as open space

    SF Planning, 2020-02-26
  • Feb 25, 2020StatusPotrero Station

    Port of San Francisco, 2020-02-20; Port of San Francisco, 2025-11-14

    Port of San Francisco, 2025-11-14; Port of San Francisco, 2020-02-20

  • 1965MilestonePotrero Station

  • ~1901MilestonePotrero Station

  • ~1901StatusPotrero Station
