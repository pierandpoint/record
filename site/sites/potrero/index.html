Sites/Potrero Station

Potrero Power Station

Former PG&E plant · Associate Capital

For decades before it was decommissioned, the Potrero Power Station supplied a significant portion of the city's energy.1 The Potrero Power Station Mixed-Use Project is opening a large portion of the city's Central Waterfront to the public for the first time in 150 years, with new homes, offices, labs, retail, light-industrial/arts uses, community facilities and nearly 7 acres of open space.1 Block 2 · UCSF is under construction; completion expected late 2028.2
