Sites/Potrero Station

Potrero Power Station

Former power plant · Fifth Space (formerly Associate Capital), developer

1 of 23 tracked complete · 1 under constructionRecord as of Oct 12, 2026

Pier & Point is an independent, cited record of waterfront development. How we work →

For decades before it was decommissioned, the Potrero Power Station supplied a significant portion of the city's energy.1 The Potrero Power Station Mixed-Use Project will open a large portion of the city's Central Waterfront to the public for the first time in 150 years, with new homes, offices, labs, retail, light-industrial/arts uses, community facilities and nearly 7 acres of open space.1 Block 2 · UCSF's Clinical and Life Sciences Building is under construction, with phased opening expected from 2028 (SF Chronicle, Jul 2026). The Board of Supervisors passed the amended and restated development agreement and the matching zoning changes on first reading on Sept 29, 2026; the Board's legislative record lists a final action on Oct 6, 2026 and shows no enactment date yet (Files 260724 and 260770, read Oct 12, 2026).2, 3, 4
