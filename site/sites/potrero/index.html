For decades before it was decommissioned, the Potrero Power Station supplied a significant portion of the city's energy.1 The Potrero Power Station Mixed-Use Project will open a large portion of the city's Central Waterfront to the public for the first time in 150 years, with new homes, offices, labs, retail, light-industrial/arts uses, community facilities and nearly 7 acres of open space.1 Block 2 · UCSF's Clinical and Life Sciences Building is under construction, with phased opening expected from 2028 (SF Chronicle, Jul 2026). The Board of Supervisors passed the amended and restated development agreement and the matching zoning changes on first reading on Sep 29, 2026 and finally on Oct 6, 2026 (11 ayes); the City's legislative record lists both as approved by the Mayor and enacted on Oct 13, 2026, Enactment Nos. 208-26 and 209-26 (Files 260724 and 260770 and the Clerk of the Board's 'Leg Final' attachments to each, read Oct 17, 2026).2, 3, 4, 5, 6
