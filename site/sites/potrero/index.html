Potrero Power Station

Former power plant · Fifth Space (formerly Associate Capital), developer

1 of 22 tracked complete · 1 under constructionRecord as of Oct 4, 2026

Pier & Point is an independent, cited record of waterfront development. How we work →

For decades before it was decommissioned, the Potrero Power Station supplied a significant portion of the city's energy.1 The Potrero Power Station Mixed-Use Project will open a large portion of the city's Central Waterfront to the public for the first time in 150 years, with new homes, offices, labs, retail, light-industrial/arts uses, community facilities and nearly 7 acres of open space.1 Block 2 · UCSF's life sciences building is under construction (started August 2025); SF YIMBY (Feb 2026) reports overall completion expected by 2030.2
