Sites/Mission Rock

Mission Rock

Port-owned land · Giants and Tishman Speyer, developers

7 of 21 tracked complete · 0 under constructionRecord as of Oct 13, 2026

Pier & Point is an independent, cited record of waterfront development. How we work →

Mission Rock's 28 acres were Seawall Lot 337, known as Oracle Park's Parking Lot A, a surface parking lot for the ballpark.1 The Giants and Tishman Speyer are developing the Port-owned site into a mixed-use neighborhood; the Port's project page describes approximately 1,200 new rental homes and 8 acres of new parks and open space.1, 2 Phase 1 was completed in 2025, including the 5-acre China Basin Park, and the City has accepted its public infrastructure; the Port Commission unanimously approved up to $10 million in Port Capital for Phase 2 pre-development on June 9, 2026, and Fieldwork Brewing opened its taproom at 300 Toni Stone Crossing in July 2026.3, 4, 5
