Sites/Mission Rock

Mission Rock

Pier 50 · Giants and Tishman Speyer

Mission Rock's 28 acres were Seawall Lot 337, known as Oracle Park's Parking Lot A, a surface parking lot for the ballpark.1 The Giants and Tishman Speyer are converting the Port-owned site into a mixed-use neighborhood of over 1.4 million square feet of office, retail and restaurant space, over 1,300 units of rental housing, and nearly 8 acres of open space and parks.1 The Canyon and Verde opened in 2024.2
