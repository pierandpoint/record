Mission Rock's project area of approximately 28 acres includes Seawall Lot 337, known as Oracle Park's Parking Lot A, a parking lot for the ballpark, together with Pier 48, China Basin Park, the marginal wharf between Piers 48 and 50, and Parcel P20.1, 2 The Giants and Tishman Speyer are developing the Port-owned site into a mixed-use neighborhood; the Port's project page describes approximately 1,200 new rental homes and 8 acres of new parks and open space, while the Port's June 2026 memo says at least 1,000 units of rental housing and SocketSite's 2019 report says over 1,300, so the planned home count differs by source.1, 3 Phase 1 was completed in 2025, including the 5-acre China Basin Park, and the City has accepted its public infrastructure; the Port Commission unanimously approved up to $10 million in Port Capital for Phase 2 pre-development on June 9, 2026, and Fieldwork Brewing opened its taproom at 300 Toni Stone Crossing in July 2026.2, 4, 5
