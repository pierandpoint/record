Sites/Mission Rock

Mission Rock

Port-owned land · Giants and Tishman Speyer, developers

Mission Rock's 28 acres were Seawall Lot 337, known as Oracle Park's Parking Lot A, a surface parking lot for the ballpark.1 The Giants and Tishman Speyer are developing the Port-owned site into a mixed-use neighborhood; the Port's project page describes approximately 1,200 new rental homes and 8 acres of new parks and open space.2 The Canyon opened in June 2023, and Verde, the fourth and final Phase 1 building, opened to residents in June 2024 and held its ribbon cutting on Sept 3, 2024.3
