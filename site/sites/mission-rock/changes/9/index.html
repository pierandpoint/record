  • Jan 30, 2018StatusMission Rock

    Mission Rock Channel Wharf: Port Commission approves the Mission Rock Design Controls (Resolution No. 18-04) and, in the same Jan 30, 2018 vote, the Disposition and Development Agreement (Resolution No. 18-03; Port minutes, Item 11.A, pp.4-5 and 19). The Design Controls name this planned open space as Channel Wharf (Design Controls Section 3.5); the Port's Jan 26, 2018 staff report requested this approval under Resolution 18-04, and its Sep 2019 memo dates the Commission's approvals January 30, 2018 (it numbers the Design Controls resolution 18-05)

    Port Commission Item 11A staff report, Jan 26 2018 (request); Port Commission Item 7A memo, Sep 20 2019 (approval date); Port Commission minutes, Jan 30, 2018 (Item 11.A, Resolution Nos. 18-03 to 18-10 adopted), https://www.sfport.com/media/3353/download
  • Jan 30, 2018MilestoneMission Rock

    Mission Rock Square: Port Commission approves the Mission Rock Disposition and Development Agreement (Resolution No. 18-03) and consents to the Special Use District zoning and approves the Design Controls (Resolution No. 18-04) that set this block's use and envelope (among Resolutions 18-03 to 18-10, which the Jan 30, 2018 minutes record as adopted; Item 11A, minutes pp.4-5 and 19)

    Port of San Francisco, Jan 30, 2018; Port of San Francisco, Jan 26, 2018; Port of San Francisco, Apr 24, 2018
  • Jan 30, 2018StatusMission Rock

    Port Commission Item 11A staff report, Jan 26 2018 (request); Port Commission Item 7A memo, Sep 20 2019 (approval date)
