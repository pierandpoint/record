  • Jun 9, 2026MilestoneMission Rock

    Mission Rock Block C: Port Commission approves Resolution 26-34, authorizing up to $10 million in Port Capital as pre-development funding for Phase 2 (for eligible Phase 2 pre-development costs, including 'design and engineering for Phase 2 infrastructure; Developer, Port, and City staff costs; consultant expenses'; the memo separately says the Developer will advance pre-development and design work for the vertical buildings in parallel), intended to avoid a 12-18 month delay to construction readiness. Staff's schedule lists a 'Potential Construction Start' of Summer/Fall 2027 for horizontal infrastructure and vertical building construction, and the memo says Phase 2 could begin construction 'as early as 2027' once anchor tenants are secured; its Table 1 gives illustrative repayment scenarios for a 2027, 2028 or 2029 vertical start – not a committed date for this or any other Phase 2 block.

    Port of San Francisco, Jun 5, 2026; Port of San Francisco, Jun 9, 2026
