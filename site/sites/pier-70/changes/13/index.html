    Pier 70 Building 104 (UIW Headquarters): Building converted to hospital use (the NRHP says the hospital 'most likely' dates from 1941; the building was already called the Navy Office building by 1938), and its rear (north) elevation infilled, giving it its present rectangular footprint

    National Park Service / California Office of Historic Preservation (nomination prepared for the Port of San Francisco by Carey & Co.), 2014-04-17
  • ~1941StatusPier 70

  • 1917StatusPier 70
