Sites/Pier 70

Pier 70

28-acre site · Port-owned · Brookfield Properties, developer

7 of 29 tracked complete · 0 under constructionRecord as of Oct 12, 2026

Pier & Point is an independent, cited record of waterfront development. How we work →

Pier 70 was headquarters to Union Iron Works and later Bethlehem Steel, and its ship-repair operations ran from the Spanish-American War in 1898 through at least 2018, per the Port's 2018 news release.1 Brookfield Properties, through FC Pier 70, LLC, is the Port's development partner for a planned 28-acre waterfront neighborhood with new housing, waterfront parks, space for artists and local manufacturing, and rehabilitated historic buildings; the Port's August 2026 update says Phase 1 vertical construction and parks have not moved forward.1, 2, 3 A one-day festival, Pier Play San Francisco, was held on Sep 19 on the future site of Elevation Sky Park, an interim entertainment venue at Pier 70 that has not yet announced an opening date.4, 5
