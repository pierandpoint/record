Pier 70 was headquarters to Union Iron Works and later Bethlehem Steel, and its ship-repair operations ran from the Spanish-American War in 1898 through at least 2018, per the Port's 2018 news release.1 Brookfield Properties, through FC Pier 70, LLC, is the Port's development partner for a planned 28-acre waterfront neighborhood with new housing, waterfront parks, space for artists and local manufacturing, and rehabilitated historic buildings; the Port's August 2026 update says Phase 1 vertical construction and parks have not moved forward.1, 2, 3 A one-day festival, Pier Play San Francisco, was held on Sep 19, 2026 on the future site of Elevation Sky Park, an interim entertainment venue at Pier 70; as of Oct 12, 2026, the venue's own page calls the site its future home and gives no opening date.4, 5
