Pier 70

28-acre site · Brookfield Properties

Pier 70 was headquarters to Union Iron Works and later Bethlehem Steel, a shipyard that repaired ships from the Spanish-American War in 1898 through the late 20th century.1 Brookfield Properties is redeveloping the Port-owned site into an expansive 28-acre waterfront neighborhood with new housing, waterfront parks, space for artists and local manufacturing, and rehabilitated historic buildings.1 Elevation Sky Park's public opening event is scheduled for Sep 19.2
