Tracked records only: nearby projects outside the four sites are left out, as in every count on this site. “Not yet complete” means planned or under construction; the two rows under it count among those records. A record standing and not yet redeveloped is not counted there.

Known gaps

Things on the four sites that are deliberately not on the map as their own shape, because no source supports drawing them honestly:

  • Unit 3 (Potrero Power Station): the former power block. It sits inside Block 9’s outline and is not drawn as a shape of its own; the Stack beside it is.
  • Seawall lots: when last searched (September 2026), DataSF’s catalogue had no layer of individual seawall lots, only the Port’s overall jurisdiction line, so they aren’t drawn as lots.

What these figures are not

They count what the record holds, not whether each claim is right. Accuracy is checked separately, claim by claim against its source, and every correction is dated and sourced on the changelog.
