Method

How the record is kept

Pier & Point tracks what is being built on San Francisco’s central waterfront, parcel by parcel, and shows where every claim comes from. Three rules govern everything on this site.

1. Nothing is fabricated

A status, date or location appears only when a primary source supports it: Port of San Francisco documents, SF Planning records, DataSF, adopted design documents, or reporting that cites them. If something can’t be confirmed, it is left off or clearly flagged, never filled in. Where we approximate — a shape traced from a planning figure, a date a source only gives as a year or a range — the record says so, in the data itself, not just here.

That’s why some shapes on the map have no dated status before today. A building may well have been under construction in 2023, but if no source says when, the map doesn’t pretend to know.

To the best of our knowledge, and true to the sources we cite.

2. Two levels of confidence

Solid shapes are real parcels from the city’s Assessor data (DataSF), joined to a cited status.

Hatched shapes are approximate. They cover land that has no Assessor parcel yet, such as future blocks in an adopted masterplan, and are traced from scaled planning figures. Each one carries its own note on how it was traced and how accurate it is likely to be. Site outlines on the overview map are drawn around the tracked records; they are not official boundaries.

3. Corrections stay visible

Every change to the record is dated and sourced on the changelog, including our own mistakes. Records are corrected, not quietly overwritten, and the full history is kept.

The timeline's window

The time slider on the map and site pages runs from 1998 to 2038, with a "before 1998" step at the left and a "2038+" step at the right for anything outside that range. 1998 is the year San Francisco adopted the Mission Bay Redevelopment Plan, when construction on the sites this record tracks began in earnest; 2038 is beyond every dated milestone currently in the record. The caps say "outside this window," not "unknown" -- a record with a real date before 1998 or after 2038 still has that date on its own page, it just isn't a separate step on the shared slider.

Where the data comes from

  • Parcels and building footprints: DataSF (City and County of San Francisco).
  • Street positions: OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Statuses, dates and approximate shapes: the documents cited on each record’s page.

Spotted something wrong?

Please tell us, ideally with a source. Confirmed corrections are credited in the changelog if you’d like.
